The Trend

The category left the table just as the season approached.

Across 12 monthly windows (Jul 2025–Jul 2026), SOMIN read organic and paid content from Pepsi and four rivals — Coca-Cola, Campa Cola, Limca and Mirinda — against a proxy panel of 7 India food creators (691 posts in a 90-day cohort). Creators put 47% of their own content into India’s culinary heritage; Pepsi published none of it, and rivals held a token 2%. At the same time, the category’s own food-pairing content decayed from 34% to 0% of a month’s content — confirmed against the prior year as a genuine decline, not a season.

  • ✓ Read 12 monthly windows of organic + paid content (Jul 2025–Jul 2026) from Pepsi and four rivals, cross-checked against the same 12 months one year earlier for every directional call.
  • ✓ Food-drink pairing & social dining decayed from 34% to 0% of category content — it ran Apr–Jul 2025 (17–25%) but was little more than a brief Iftar-window echo (16%, Mar–Apr) in 2026.
  • ✓ Sports fandom togetherness & access is the category’s sustained, event-cyclic baseline — 0% to 88% of a month’s content, peaking with the FIFA World Cup (Jun–Jul 2026); the Olympics, Champions Trophy and IPL drove the same peaks the prior year.
  • ✓ Festive communal celebration is genuinely seasonal, verified across 2 cycles — it peaks 38–39% Sep–Jan both years (58% Aug–Sep ‘24, 50% Dec–Jan, 36% Holi).
  • ✓ Pepsi’s own organic output already leans Brand Offering (87 of 119 items this year); paid stays thin at 12 ads and mirrors organic’s product focus — no food-pairing thread runs anywhere yet.

About this case study.

What is a SOMIN brand briefing?
A data-backed content strategy built from a real read of a brand’s category. SOMIN analyzes a year or more of organic and paid content from the brand and its named competitors, plus a content-proxy panel of relevant creators, then turns the patterns into trend analysis, whitespace mapping, content pillars, a publishing calendar and a creative library. This example covers 12 months of content from Pepsi, Coca-Cola, Campa Cola, Limca and Mirinda, read against a 7-creator Foodie panel in India.
What is whitespace analysis in marketing?
It compares how much a brand, its competitors and a relevant creator panel each publish on a content theme — as a share of their own cohort, never raw counts, since panel sizes can differ by more than 27x. Here, SOMIN scored seven territories across the India cola category and found three where Pepsi has real permission to lead: culinary heritage, indulgence-health balance, and festive food togetherness.
What does “prior-year verified” mean?
Every directional trend in this briefing — decayed, seasonal, or sustained — was re-run against the same 12 months one year earlier before being called a genuine trend rather than a single-cycle slope. Food-drink pairing content’s decline, for example, was confirmed against 2025 data: it ran through spring and summer that year but only echoed briefly around Iftar in 2026.
What’s included in a SOMIN-generated content calendar?
Specific posting slots mapped to real SOMIN moment triggers drawn from the underlying trend, whitespace and insight-chain data. Pepsi’s calendar runs 39 posts across 13 weeks, weighted 50/30/20% across three pillars and anchored to verified cultural moments like Onam and Diwali on their real calendar dates, not a generic template.
How long does it take to build a content strategy with SOMIN?
This briefing — a year of category research across 5 cola brands and 7 creators, three whitespace-backed content pillars, a 13-week/39-post calendar, and a 108-piece creative library — is the kind of work SOMIN’s platform typically completes 13x faster than manual research workflows.
