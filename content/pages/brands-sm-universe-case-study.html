FAQ

SM Universe case study FAQ

What is a SOMIN brand briefing?
A data-backed read of a brand's category from real organic content, not surveys, turned into trends, whitespace scoring, pillars, a calendar and a creative library.
What is whitespace analysis, and how many cohorts does SOMIN score it against?
The share of its own content a brand, its competitors and a creator panel give each theme, not raw counts. Four territories were rated recover, contested, blocked or emerging.
What is a creator panel, and why track it separately from competitors?
Real creators posting to the same audience, tracked apart from brands to show what independent voices publish, not what the audience ‘wants.’ SM Universe's panel of vocal coaches and K-pop creators made craft 79% of its singing content, a signal no academy showed.
What's included in the SM Universe content calendar?
39 posts over 13 weeks (Jul 6–Oct 4, 2026), Tuesday/Thursday/Saturday, weighted 20/12/7 toward Own the Craft around Singapore dates such as National Day.
