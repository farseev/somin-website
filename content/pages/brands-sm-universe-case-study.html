The Trend

Supply nearly tripled. The conversation is loudest right now.

SM Universe's audience is a Singapore teen-to-young-adult who treats the voice as identity — K-pop-fluent, training-hungry, chasing skill, recognition and a place to perform. SOMIN calls this primary persona The Aspiring Singer. Across 24 months, the Singing conversation they live in nearly tripled: 74 organic items per window in mid-2024, 213 by Q2 2026 — the loudest window on record. Two themes carry most of it today: stage & access commons (51% of the latest window) and K-pop training & audition pathway (48%, arriving from 0% since SM Universe's own August 2025 entry). A third theme — vocal technique & coaching quality — fell to 0% of what academies publish, even as SOMIN's 10-creator panel built it into 79% of their own Singing content.

  • ✓ Read 24 months of organic Singing content (Jun 2024–Jun 2026) — 1,306 items — across SM Universe, 11 named Singapore academies (K-Pop Vocal Coach, JR Academy, VOCA Academy, The Vocal Studio and more), and a 10-creator panel.
  • ✓ Stage & access commons is the sustained default — peaking at 96% of the window (2025 Q3), still 51% of the latest one — but it's 61% competitor-owned and SM Universe holds 0% of it: staying out is the right call, not a gap.
  • ✓ K-pop training & audition pathway is verified genuine emergence, not a seasonal build — the same months in Y1 measured 0%. It arrived with SM Universe's own entry in August 2025 and settled at 40–48% of the category conversation, SM Universe already holding 96% share of it.
  • ✓ Vocal technique & coaching quality is a verified real decline for academies — lower at every matched month year-over-year (14%→4% average) — even as the 10-creator panel's own singing voice switched on in December 2025 and built craft into 79% of what it publishes.
  • ✓ Brand Offering already leads the category's organic Singing content at 266 items in the latest 90-day window, Community Engagement 156, Educational only 101 — technique-led educational content is the open lane, not a from-scratch plan.
The Whitespace

Eleven academies compete for Singing. None owns the voice itself.

SOMIN scored four content territories across three cohorts — SM Universe (77 items/90D), 11 named competitors (220 items/90D, 2.9× the size of SM Universe's own), and a 10-creator content-proxy panel (118 items/90D) — comparing each cohort's own share, never raw counts. Three territories became this briefing's pillars; a fourth, competitor-owned commons, stays a defensive watch, not a lead.

Questions

About this case study.

What is a SOMIN brand briefing?
A data-backed read of a brand's category built from real organic content, not survey data. For SM Universe, SOMIN analyzed 24 months of Singing-tagged organic content (Jun 2024–Jun 2026) from SM Universe, 11 named Singapore academies, and a 10-creator content-proxy panel, then turned the patterns into trend analysis, three-cohort whitespace scoring, three content pillars, a publishing calendar, and a 72-piece creative library.
What is whitespace analysis, and how many cohorts does SOMIN score it against?
Whitespace analysis compares how much of its own content a brand, its competitors, and a creator panel devote to a given theme — as a share of each cohort's own output, never raw counts, since one cohort can be several times the size of another. For SM Universe, SOMIN scored four territories across three cohorts — SM Universe (77 items/90D), 11 competitors (220 items/90D), and a 10-creator panel (118 items/90D) — and verdicted each as recover, contested, blocked, or emerging.
What is a creator panel, and why track it separately from competitors?
A creator panel is a content-proxy cohort — real creators posting in the same audience space — tracked separately from named competitor brands because it shows what independent voices are already publishing, not what the audience ‘wants.’ For SM Universe, SOMIN tracked a 10-creator panel of vocal coaches and K-pop creators; its Singing output emerged in December 2025 and built vocal-technique craft into 79% of what it publishes, a signal no competing academy showed.
What's included in the SM Universe content calendar?
39 posts across 13 weeks (Jul 6–Oct 4, 2026), on a Tuesday/Thursday/Saturday cadence with Tuesday lead posts, weighted 20/12/7 across the three pillars — roughly 50/30/20, favoring Own the Craft — and anchored to Singapore's own calendar: National Day, Teachers' Day, the September school holidays and the Mid-Autumn Festival.
