At SOMIN, we envision a world where advertisers are empowered to work with purpose, balance, and excellence, free from the pressures of time constraints, error anxiety, and complexity in data. We believe that true success in advertising stems from fostering well-being, creativity, and integrity in service to clients and communities.

SOMIN is committed to creating products that ease the burdens on advertisers and marketing data analysts, equipping them with tools that maximize efficiency, clarity, and confidence in their roles. We stand with our users as they seek to bring honest, impactful narratives to their clients while reclaiming time and space for family, faith, and well-being.

Through SOMIN, we aim to transform the advertising industry, creating a ripple effect of positive change, where technology serves not just as a tool for profit, but as a force for balance, peace of mind, and meaningful connection in the workplace and beyond.

Faith-driven leadership

Our vision is redemptive marketing

The values above have a name: redemptive marketing. In a Forbes Technology Council essay, our co-founder and CEO, Prof. Aleks Farseev, traces the mistrust between advertisers and agencies to metrics chosen to look good rather than to sell. His answer is honest measurement, AI that pre-evaluates ads before budget is spent, and partnerships built on redemptive entrepreneurship, where “sometimes you need to sacrifice for your partner or client”.

The faith behind the vision is public. In a February 2024 profile, Salt&Light tells how a child-like prayer for ducks laid the faith foundation for an AI start-up — the story of Prof. Aleks Farseev and how his Christian faith shapes the way he builds SOMIN (SoMin.ai).

We live it through two communities. SOMIN is part of the Faith Driven Entrepreneur group, and Prof. Farseev leads faithdriven.sg — Faith Driven Singapore, the network's Singapore chapter, which runs the eight-session Foundations programme for Christian founders, business owners and professionals. The second is orthodox.sg, an unofficial website for the Dormition parish of the Russian Orthodox Church in Singapore, an Orthodox Christian community on Highland Road, near Kovan MRT.
