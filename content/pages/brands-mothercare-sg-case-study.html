The audience

GWI data shows Singapore mothers trust peers over broadcast

Singapore mothers rely more on trust and community validation than the average Singaporean: peer opinions, reviews and expert endorsement outweigh brand announcements. The channels furthest above the national baseline are celebrity or influencer endorsements (index 147.3) and social media recommendations or comments (index 131.0).
