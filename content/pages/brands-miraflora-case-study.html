The Brand

A challenger CBD brand, up against bigger budgets.

Miraflora sells CBD products into a crowded US wellness category — including its +Balance Peach Ginger Sparkling Beverage, infused with 35mg of full-spectrum CBD per can plus added vitamins and minerals. Internally, the read on Miraflora’s position was blunt: it was fighting brands with far more traction, and its own posting decisions leaned on vanity metrics rather than a defined read of what was actually working. The brief SOMIN was given was specific — stop eyeballing which posts look like the “best” and “worst” performers, and build a brand-specific, statistical way to find genuine outliers before deciding what to boost.

  • ✓ Category: CBD Edibles, led by its +Balance Peach Ginger Sparkling Beverage (35mg full-spectrum CBD per can).
  • ✓ Brand archetypes tagged The Caregiver and The Magician — tone of voice: Playful, Inviting, Relaxing.
  • ✓ Core human needs it plays to: Health & Wellness, Stability, Comfort, Relaxation.
  • ✓ Addressable problem: stress and lack of relaxation in daily life, occasion-tagged to mid-week relaxation and stressful moments.

Why It Resonates

Pet-stress content isn’t random — it maps to seven real psychological drivers.

SOMIN paired its two anxiety-linked addressable-problem themes — Pet Anxiety & Stress (12 posts) and Pet Stress Management around holidays and fireworks (14 posts) — with the psychological mechanisms actually driving them.

  • ✓ Fear Conditioning — pets can develop fear responses to stimuli like fireworks or thunderstorms from past negative experiences or under-socialization.
  • ✓ Separation Anxiety — pets grow distressed when separated from owners, especially during holiday travel or loud festivities.
  • ✓ Social Learning — pets pick up cues from an owner’s behavior, learning to associate certain events or environments with stress.
  • ✓ Generalized Anxiety Disorder — some pets are predisposed to broader anxiety, making them more stress-prone in loud or unfamiliar situations.
  • ✓ Lack of Predictability — pets thrive on routine, so sudden holiday or travel changes cause stress and uncertainty.
  • ✓ Inadequate Environmental Enrichment — low mental stimulation leaves pets more prone to boredom and anxiety.
  • ✓ Owner Stress Transfer — pets are sensitive to their owners’ emotions and can mirror or absorb that stress, especially during high-activity periods.
Key Findings

Three things we’d stake the work on.

01

Miraflora’s own team had already diagnosed the real problem: chasing likes doesn’t tell you what to boost next. What actually matters is which posts perform outside Miraflora’s own normal range — a brand-specific statistical outlier, not a category-wide “top performer” list picked by eye.

02

Miraflora’s content already speaks the same language as Recess (83.97% average content-verse match) and BRĒZ (83.64%) — especially on what customers need from a CBD brand, where BRĒZ aligns at 97.50%, the single highest score in the whole set. Prima sits apart at just 68.57%: evidence that “more CBD content” isn’t one playbook — there’s a real content-verse Miraflora fits inside, and one it doesn’t.

03

The single largest content theme in the read (31 posts) is pet owners seeking natural solutions for their pets’ comfort — a direct bridge to a CBD wellness message. Turning that into a repeatable program means closing two measurement gaps Miraflora didn’t have yet — page growth and day-by-day engagement velocity — then reading evergreen, seasonal, and topical content through the same brand-specific outlier lens.

Questions

About this case study.

What is a SOMIN content-verse benchmark?
It scores cosine similarity between a brand’s content and its named competitors’ content across four dimensions — audiences, interests, aspirations, and customer needs — generated using an Azure LLM and SOMIN’s SODA pipeline. For Miraflora, SOMIN benchmarked its content-verse against seven named CBD rivals: BRĒZ, Charlotte’s Web, Equilibria Women, Feals, Medterra, Prima, and Recess.
How does SOMIN find outlier content instead of just “best performing” posts?
Rather than eyeballing which posts have the most likes, SOMIN scopes a brand-specific standard-deviation model that flags posts performing meaningfully outside a brand’s own normal range — genuine outliers, not a hand-picked top-10 list. For Miraflora this was framed as a direct fix for a real problem: deciding what to boost without leaning on vanity metrics.
What is SOMIN’s theme detection?
SOMIN clusters organic and paid content into recurring themes and the addressable problems behind them. In this read of 2,745 pieces of CBD-category content (2,219 organic, 526 paid), theme detection surfaced a distinct pet-wellness thread — including a 31-post theme on pet owners seeking natural solutions for their pets’ comfort, the single largest theme in the set.
Are the story concepts in this case study real customer testimonials?
No. SOMIN’s story engine pairs a detected theme with the psychological factor behind it, then expands it into a full narrative concept using an LLM — a creative starting point for a brand’s own content, not an account from a real pet owner. The concept featured on this page, “Echoes of Emotion,” was generated from the Owner Stress Transfer factor behind Miraflora’s Pet Stress Management theme.
What’s next for Miraflora’s content measurement?
SOMIN scoped two tracking gaps to close next — page growth (likes and follower growth over time) and engagement velocity (day-by-day engagement) — neither of which was being captured yet. Once in place, the same brand-specific outlier model gets applied across evergreen, seasonal, and topical content.
