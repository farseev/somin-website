Why it resonates

Seven psychological drivers behind pet-stress content

Two anxiety themes, Pet Anxiety & Stress (12 posts) and Pet Stress Management around holidays and fireworks (14 posts), and the mechanisms behind them.

  • ✓ Fear Conditioning — past bad experiences or under-socialization teach fear of fireworks or thunderstorms.
  • ✓ Separation Anxiety — distress when apart from owners, especially during holiday travel or loud festivities.
  • ✓ Social Learning — pets learn from owners’ behavior to link certain events or places with stress.
  • ✓ Generalized Anxiety Disorder — a predisposition to broader anxiety, worse in loud or unfamiliar settings.
  • ✓ Lack of Predictability — pets need routine, so sudden holiday or travel changes cause stress.
  • ✓ Inadequate Environmental Enrichment — low mental stimulation leaves pets more prone to boredom and anxiety.
  • ✓ Owner Stress Transfer — pets mirror or absorb their owners’ stress, especially in busy periods.
Key findings

Three Miraflora findings on outliers, rivals and pet wellness

01

Miraflora’s team knew likes do not show what to boost next. Posts performing outside the brand’s own normal range do: statistical outliers, not a list picked by eye.

02

Miraflora is closest to Recess (83.97% average match) and BRĒZ (83.64%), above all on customer needs, where BRĒZ scores 97.50%, the set’s highest. Prima sits apart at 68.57%: there is a content-verse Miraflora fits and one it does not.

03

The largest theme (31 posts), pet owners seeking natural comfort for their pets, bridges directly to CBD wellness. Making it repeatable needs page growth and engagement velocity tracking, then the same outlier lens on evergreen, seasonal and topical content.

FAQ

Miraflora case study FAQ

What is a SOMIN content-verse benchmark?
Cosine similarity between a brand’s content and named competitors’ on audiences, interests, aspirations and customer needs, via an Azure LLM and SOMIN’s SODA pipeline. Miraflora’s set: BRĒZ, Charlotte’s Web, Equilibria Women, Feals, Medterra, Prima and Recess.
How does SOMIN find outlier content instead of just “best performing” posts?
A brand-specific standard-deviation model flags posts performing meaningfully outside the brand’s normal range, so Miraflora can decide what to boost without vanity metrics.
What is SOMIN’s theme detection?
SOMIN clusters organic and paid content into recurring themes and the problems behind them. Here it surfaced a pet-wellness thread led by the 31-post theme on natural comfort for pets.
Are the story concepts in this case study real customer testimonials?
No. SOMIN’s story engine expands a detected theme and its psychological factor into a narrative with an LLM: a creative starting point, not a real pet owner’s account.
What’s next for Miraflora’s content measurement?
Two metrics not yet tracked: page growth (likes and followers over time) and engagement velocity (day-by-day engagement). Then the same outlier model applies to evergreen, seasonal and topical content.
