The Client

A global media network’s performance practice in the Middle East.

Havas Media is one of the world’s largest media agency networks, with practices spanning paid, programmatic, and performance media across every major market. The team featured here is the Performance Practice based in the Middle East, led by Performance Director Branislav, which manages multi-brand media planning and buying across the region’s most competitive consumer categories.

The Challenge

Performance planning at network scale was buried under manual data work.

Performance media planning at agency network scale means juggling many client brands across many platforms, each with its own data layer. Manually collating real-time competitor activity across paid and organic creative is the kind of work that quietly burns hours and starves strategic thinking of the time it deserves.

  • ✓ Many client brands, many platforms — each with its own data layer to track by hand.
  • ✓ Real-time competitor activity across paid and organic creative had to be manually collated before planning could even start.
  • ✓ Every new brief meant re-establishing competitive context from scratch — pulling and interpreting a category’s worth of data.
  • ✓ The work was rigorous, but it wasn’t fast — and “fast” matters when a new account expects a media strategy in week one.
The Approach

A real-time competitive layer underneath every client brand.

Havas Media’s Middle East performance team integrated SOMIN as the real-time competitive read across their book of business. SOMIN’s Brand Tracker pulls competitor content automatically and breaks it down by activity, audience, creative territory, and seasonality — giving planners an always-on view of where each client brand stands versus its competitive set, refreshed continuously.

For new client onboarding, the team uses SOMIN to instantly establish factual context about a brand and its category before the media strategy is committed. Where the previous workflow required hours of manual research before the first planning conversation, SOMIN delivers a usable category baseline in minutes.

The Outcome

From a tool the team uses, to a tool the team depends on.

SOMIN has become an integral part of Havas Media’s daily operations in the region — not a tool reached for occasionally, but one the team has come to depend on. Real-time competitive access replaces hours of manual cross-platform filtering, and conversations with clients are sharper because they’re anchored in current competitive evidence rather than dated category audits.

The runway from “new client signed” to “media strategy active” has shortened materially, because the competitive context is in hand by day one — the kind of turnaround consistent with SOMIN’s typical 13× speed-up over manual research workflows.

Key Takeaways

What this case study actually proves.

01

Manual, cross-platform competitor tracking is a hidden tax on performance media at network scale — it doesn’t show up on a media plan, but it eats the hours planners need for strategic thinking. Automating that read is what turned SOMIN from a nice-to-have into daily infrastructure.

02

New client onboarding is a speed problem as much as a research problem. When a new account expects a media strategy in week one, the value isn’t just accuracy — it’s having a factual category baseline ready on day one instead of after days of manual digging.

03

“Depend on,” not “reach for occasionally,” is the real adoption signal. Havas Media didn’t add SOMIN as an extra tool in the stack — it replaced the manual workflow that used to sit between a signed client and an active strategy.
