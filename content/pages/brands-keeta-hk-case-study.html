The persona

The Flavourful Culinary Enthusiast and two pain points

Focus Persona

Flavourful Culinary Enthusiast

A passionate foodie who loves exploring different flavors, cooking delicious meals, and finding great deals on food and cooking products. Aspires to happiness, financial stability and a healthy lifestyle — needs built around comfort, well-being and a sense of belonging.

Targeted by: FoodPanda & Deliveroo · above-average predicted CTR on Purchase-optimized ads
Focus Pain Point I

Affordable & Convenient Food Options

The brands are addressing the common consumer tension of the need for affordable and convenient food options, especially during festive periods and for families and friends — alongside a desire for healthier choices and convenient access to diverse, delicious meals.

Dominated by: FoodPanda · Addressed by: Deliveroo · Keeta lagging
Focus Pain Point II

Access to Affordable, Convenient Food

Tensions revolve around the high cost of food, the inconvenience of ordering and receiving it, and the challenge of finding affordable, convenient options — consumers frustrated by late deliveries, high food costs, and the overall inconvenience of the ordering process.

The rewrite

GWI-refined lines swap price talk for curation and quality

SOMIN cross-checked every AI-generated line against a GWI data snapshot of Hong Kong food orderers in the above-average income bracket. An over-indexed trait is significantly more common in that audience than in the general population; an under-indexed trait is less common. Story after story, tone after tone, this audience under-indexed on budget and value-driven behavior and over-indexed on curated, quality-first choices — so SOMIN rewrote the direct-to-action line in every story to match.

Story & Tone Raw AI Line GWI-Refined Line Why It Changed
Story I · Direct-to-action “Faster, fairer, and always on time—Keeta delivers what others don’t.” “Curated, quick, and always hot—Keeta gets it right, every time.” Replaces affordability with curation and quality — this audience dislikes mass-market fast food.
Story II · Direct-to-action “Quick to your door, kind to your wallet—Keeta makes every bite worth it.” “Smart meals for smart people—Keeta delivers fast, curated, and made for you.” Removes “cheap” and “worth it” language, which doesn’t align with this audience’s low price-sensitivity.
Story III · Direct-to-action “Fresh, fair, and always nearby—Keeta bridges the gap to good food.” “No fluff, no fuss—Keeta delivers curated meals fast, with zero compromise.” Removes “fair” and “bridging food gaps,” which leaned toward under-indexed budget and access messaging.
Story IV · Direct-to-action “Keeta cuts the wait, not the flavor—fast delivery without the price sting.” “Hot, fast, and no shortcuts—Keeta delivers meals that feel like they were made for you.” Replaces price and “cutting flavor” language with quality and speed — more motivating for this audience.

Key findings

Three Keeta HK findings before a single ad goes live

01

Keeta was trailing FoodPanda and Deliveroo on the exact tension both rivals had already claimed as their own — affordable, convenient food — the category’s single most contested pain point.

02

The persona behind that tension — Hong Kong food orderers in the above-average income bracket — is repeatedly under-indexed on budget-conscious, value-driven behavior and over-indexed on curated, quality-first choices, across all four AI-generated stories’ GWI validation.

03

The fix wasn’t a new persona or a new story — it was removing “affordable,” “fair” and “price” language from every direct-to-action line and replacing it with curation, quality and personalization, story by story, tone by tone.
