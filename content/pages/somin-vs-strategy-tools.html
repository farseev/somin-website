The short answer

These tools answer four different questions. ChatGPT and Perplexity answer what does the internet know. Meltwater answers what is being said about brands. GWI answers who the audience is, from representative survey data. SOMIN answers what is winning in your category's creative right now -- and what to say next: competitor ads decoded for proposition and tension, the whitespace mapped, concepts pre-scored, rendered as a client-ready deck. Most strategy teams pair them: define the audience in GWI, watch the conversation in Meltwater, decide the angle in SOMIN.

Head to head

Fair comparison -- including when to pick them.

SOMIN vs ChatGPT

ChatGPT is an extraordinary generalist for reasoning, drafting, and exploration -- but it answers from general knowledge, not your live category, and can't decode competitor creative or score a concept. SOMIN is purpose-built for category strategy, with predictive scoring and client-ready output.

Choose ChatGPT if

You want to explore, brainstorm, and draft across any topic, fast and cheaply.

Choose SOMIN if

You need to decide from live category signal -- and pitch it -- with scoring and a client-ready deck.

SOMIN vs Perplexity

Perplexity is excellent at sourced web research with citations. SOMIN answers a different question -- not "what does the web say" but "what's winning in this category, and where's the whitespace" -- grounded in decoded competitor creative, with a deck you can hand a client.

Choose Perplexity if

You want cited, general desk research across the open web.

Choose SOMIN if

You want category-grounded strategy and pitch-ready output, not a web summary.

SOMIN vs Meltwater

Meltwater is a deep media and social intelligence suite -- monitoring news, social, forums and reviews across a huge footprint, with share of voice, sentiment, crisis detection and executive reporting. That breadth of monitoring is genuinely hard to match. SOMIN isn't monitoring what's said about you; it decodes what brands are doing -- every competitor ad parsed for proposition, persona and tension -- then maps the whitespace and pre-scores the concepts to run.

Choose Meltwater if

You need broad media monitoring, reputation tracking, share of voice and comms reporting.

Choose SOMIN if

You need to decide the creative angle from what's winning in-category -- and pitch it.

SOMIN vs GWI

GWI is representative consumer research -- a large, ongoing survey across many markets, with deep profiling of attitudes, media habits and behaviours. For defining and validating an audience with defensible sample sizes, that's its own category, and SOMIN doesn't replicate it. SOMIN works on the other half of the brief: what's landing in the category's creative right now, refreshed live rather than on a survey cycle, and what to say next. Many teams use GWI to define the audience and SOMIN to decide the angle.

Choose GWI if

You need representative, survey-grade audience data to define and defend who you're targeting.

Choose SOMIN if

You need the live creative picture -- what's winning, what's open, and what to run.

How is SOMIN different from Meltwater?
Meltwater monitors what's being said -- mentions, sentiment, share of voice across news and social, at real breadth. SOMIN decodes what brands are doing: competitor ads parsed for proposition, persona and tension, the whitespace mapped, and concepts pre-scored. Different jobs; they sit together well.
Does SOMIN replace GWI's audience data?
No. GWI's representative survey data is its own category, and SOMIN doesn't replicate that sample-backed rigour. SOMIN covers the live creative half of the brief -- what's landing in-category now and what to say. Teams commonly define the audience in GWI and decide the angle in SOMIN.
