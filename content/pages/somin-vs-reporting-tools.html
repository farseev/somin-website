SOMIN vs Talkwalker

Talkwalker is enterprise listening and analytics -- mentions, sentiment, and trends at scale. SOMIN reports a different thing: what's actually landing in the category, decomposed into themes, as the client's own live report in their language.

Choose Talkwalker if

You need large-scale listening and sentiment analytics.

Choose SOMIN if

You want a themed, always-current client report on what's actually working.

SOMIN vs Rival IQ

Rival IQ is competitive social benchmarking -- how you stack up on metrics against a defined set of rivals. SOMIN goes past the metrics to what's resonating and why, themed and self-serve for the client.

Choose Rival IQ if

You need head-to-head social benchmarking on metrics.

Choose SOMIN if

You want to show clients what's landing, decomposed and live between reviews.

SOMIN vs Whatagraph

Whatagraph automates multi-source marketing dashboards and client reports -- great for pulling many channels into one branded report. SOMIN reports the category layer it doesn't: what's landing by theme, from the same engine that decodes categories, live between reviews.

Choose Whatagraph if

You need to automate multi-channel reporting into branded reports.

Choose SOMIN if

You want themed category reporting the client can open themselves.
