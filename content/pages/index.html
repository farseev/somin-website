Every chain in this game comes from a real SOMIN case study. Build it step by step and I'll show you why each move works. Step one is the persona: who the conversation is really about. SOMIN clusters thousands of real posts and ads into personas, then turns each into a named character enriched with traits from those posts. Next, the pain point (SOMIN calls it a tension): the conflict people keep posting about. SOMIN measures how much of the conversation each one holds for the brand, its competitors and creators, so you can see where to claim, differentiate or stay out. Now pick the angle: the lens that makes this pain point worth listening to. SOMIN generates candidates grounded in validated theory (psychological, sociological, economic or behavioural-economic) and the strategist picks the one that fits. Not this one: that angle was built for another pain point in the same study. Ask which lens explains why this person feels this pain. Here is the insight, which SOMIN calls a story. It fuses the character, the pain point and the angle into one human truth: the big idea every proposition, ad and post is built from. Time to ship. Propositions pair the insight with the product's features, reasons to believe, USP and offers; expressions turn them into creatives for each funnel stage. TOFU builds awareness, MOFU earns consideration, BOFU converts, and the SEO article meets people already searching. Before launch, SOMIN can score each creative's predicted clickability and plan a dated calendar from real moments, so the chain you just built becomes a plan you can run. Not in this case study

Questions

SOMIN FAQ: What teams ask first.

How is SOMIN different from social listening?
Listening tools report what people say; SOMIN shows what works and turns it into briefs.
How is it different from ChatGPT?
ChatGPT draws on the open web; SOMIN uses verified category data and cites its sources.
Who is SOMIN for?
Agencies: pitches, reporting and strategy (agency playbook); brands: competitor tracking, briefs and on-brand content (brand playbook); creator programs: choosing and briefing creators (creator playbook).
Where does the data come from?
Public ad libraries and posts plus licensed partner data, with no gated scraping, handled on Google Cloud under the GDPR and Singapore's PDPA.
How fast will we see results?
Teams start on day one; a brief becomes scored ideas in about 13 minutes (Google Cloud's customer story).
