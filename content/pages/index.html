    Questions

    SOMIN FAQ: What teams ask first.

    How is SOMIN different from a social listening tool?
    Listening tools tell you what people say. SOMIN shows you what is working: which messages competitors push, which gaps they leave open. Then it turns that into ideas and briefs you can use.
    How is it different from ChatGPT?
    ChatGPT answers from the open web and can change its answer each time. SOMIN answers from verified data about your own category, follows a fixed research process, and shows the posts behind every insight.
    Who is SOMIN for?
    Agencies use it to pitch, report and plan strategy (agency playbook). Brands use it to watch competitors, brief their teams and create on-brand content (brand playbook). Creator programs use it to choose creators and brief them to sell (creator playbook).
    Where does the data come from?
    Public ad libraries and posts from Meta, Google, TikTok and YouTube, plus licensed partner data. No gated scraping. Data handling follows the GDPR and Singapore's PDPA, on Google Cloud.
    How fast will we see results?
    Most teams are working in SOMIN on day one. A typical brief becomes scored ideas in about 13 minutes, as documented in Google Cloud's customer story.

    Which markets do you cover?
    11+ markets across APAC, MENA and Europe, including Singapore, Malaysia, Indonesia, the Philippines, Vietnam, India, the UAE, Saudi Arabia, the UK, Germany and Spain. New markets take about two weeks.
