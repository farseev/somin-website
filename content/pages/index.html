    Questions

    SOMIN FAQ: What teams ask first.

    How is SOMIN different from social listening?
    Listening tools report what people say; SOMIN shows what works and turns it into briefs.
    How is it different from ChatGPT?
    ChatGPT draws on the open web; SOMIN uses verified category data and cites its sources.
    Who is SOMIN for?
    Agencies: pitches, reporting and strategy (agency playbook); brands: competitor tracking, briefs and on-brand content (brand playbook); creator programs: choosing and briefing creators (creator playbook).
    Where does the data come from?
    Public ad libraries and posts plus licensed partner data, with no gated scraping, handled on Google Cloud under the GDPR and Singapore's PDPA.
    How fast will we see results?
    Teams start on day one; a brief becomes scored ideas in about 13 minutes (Google Cloud's customer story).
    Is grant support available in Singapore?
    SMEs are eligible for up to 50% business grant support for the adoption of SOMIN, a Pre-Approved Solution under the IMDA SMEs Go Digital programme. Check your eligibility →
    Which markets do you cover?
    11+ markets, including Singapore, Malaysia, Indonesia, the Philippines, Vietnam, India, the UAE, Saudi Arabia, the UK, Germany and Spain. New markets take about two weeks.
