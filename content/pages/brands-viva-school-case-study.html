The trend

The pillars

Three VIVA pillars: the whole artist, real craft and strength

Competitor absence alone isn’t permission — each pillar rests on a real Viva asset: the only dual music-and-ballet curriculum in the set, RAD certification plus the Vaganova method behind the craft claim, and Viva’s own mission — music and dance that “develop the mind and refine the soul” — behind the strength reframe.

Pillar 1 of 3 · Partial, brand-led · 38% weight

The Whole Artist

The play: parents want their child to grow as a complete artist, but every other school in the set offers only dance, forcing a choice between music and movement. Viva is the only school teaching both music (violin, piano, guitar) and ballet, for children and adults — the “whole artist” is literally what Viva is. Insight: “Art bridges the gap between expectations and self-expression.”

Tension: Bridging the Gap in Arts Education (music + ballet) · Evidence: 15 of 26 evidence posts, 46 engagement, 65% brand share-of-voice
Pillar 2 of 3 · Emerging · 31% weight

Real Craft, Real Progression

The play: generic “ballet classes” are everywhere, but parents serious about their child’s development can’t easily tell genuine classical training from a recital factory. RAD certification (the UK’s foremost dance examining body) plus the Vaganova Russian method are verifiable credentials competitors rarely foreground. This pathway also reaches the underserved Adult Returner — competitors skew to early childhood; Viva’s “it’s never too late” is built for them. Insight: “Beauty is born from resilience, not just poise.”

Tension: Beyond the Surface: the true nature of ballet · Evidence: 38 of 1,117 evidence posts, 5,855 engagement, 0% brand share-of-voice
Pillar 3 of 3 · Contested · 31% weight

The Strength Behind the Grace

The play: ballet is seen as delicate and graceful, but what parents and students value most is the strength, discipline and confidence it builds. Viva’s own mission is exactly this territory — Viva simply hasn’t voiced it yet. Insight: “Embrace vulnerability; it is the soil where true strength takes root.”

Tension: Embracing Vulnerability and Strength in Dance · Evidence: 87 of 1,117 evidence posts, 14,783 engagement — highest in the set

This builds on real momentum, not a blank slate: category content already runs hottest in ballet-training access (667 items) and strength & expression (405 items) — but it rarely reaches paid media, where Viva spends nothing today. The recommended shift leans Viva’s own voice into all three pillars, starting from where the category’s attention already sits.

Key findings

Three moves for VIVA, ranked by how fast the ground is closing

01

The Strength Behind the Grace: the strength/expression theme was near-absent until 2026, then surged to category-leading volume (0→190 posts per window, prior-year verified) as competitors rushed in. It’s contested but not yet entrenched — and Viva is absent. The window to own a distinct angle is open but closing.

02

The Whole Artist: across two years, music sits near-zero in category content — and only Viva ever publishes it; all four competitors publish zero. The space is Viva’s almost by default, and it’s literally what makes Viva different: the only school in the set teaching both music and ballet.

03

Real Craft, Real Progression: the ‘true nature of ballet’ — craft and discipline — is barely published by anyone (competitors 3%, Viva ~0%), while the generic training-access commons is blocked: competitors out-publish Viva there roughly 40:1. The craft sub-niche is open, and Viva can evidence it — RAD certification and the Vaganova method — where others can’t.

FAQ

VIVA case study FAQ

What is a SOMIN brand briefing?
A data-backed content strategy built from a real read of a brand’s category. SOMIN analyzes organic and paid content from the brand and its named competitors, then turns the patterns into trend analysis, whitespace mapping, content pillars, a publishing calendar and a creative library. This example covers two years (Jun 2024–Jun 2026) of content from Viva School of Music & Ballet and four Singapore rivals — The Ballet School, Singapore Ballet Academy, City Ballet Academy and Cheng Ballet Academy.
What is whitespace analysis in marketing?
It compares how much a brand and its competitors publish on a content theme, as a proportional share of their own content rather than raw counts — necessary here because Viva’s tracked content (26 items) and the competitor cohort (1,117 items) differ 43×. SOMIN scored five territories for Viva and found three where it has real permission to act: music, classical craft, and the strength/confidence reframe.
What does “prior-year verified” mean?
Every directional trend in this briefing — rising, emerging, decayed or seasonal — was checked against the same two-month window one year earlier before being reported as a genuine trend rather than a single-cycle spike. Ballet-training access & quality was confirmed up year-on-year in 6 of 6 checked periods; the strength theme’s 2026 surge (0→190 in the Apr–Jun window) was confirmed against the same window a year earlier.
What’s included in a SOMIN-generated content calendar?
Specific posting slots mapped to real SOMIN moment triggers drawn from the underlying trend, whitespace and insight-chain data. Viva’s calendar runs 39 posts across 13 weeks in contiguous pillar blocks — The Whole Artist, then The Strength Behind the Grace, then Real Craft, Real Progression — anchored to real dates including Singapore’s National Day.
