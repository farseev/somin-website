The pillars

Three VIVA pillars: the whole artist, real craft and strength

Competitor absence is not permission; each pillar rests on a Viva asset: the set’s only dual music-and-ballet curriculum, RAD certification plus the Vaganova method, and Viva’s mission of music and dance that “develop the mind and refine the soul”.

Pillar 1 of 3 · Partial, brand-led · 38% weight

The Whole Artist

The play: parents want a complete artist, but every other school offers only dance. Viva alone teaches music (violin, piano, guitar) and ballet, for children and adults. Insight: “Art bridges the gap between expectations and self-expression.”

Tension: Bridging the Gap in Arts Education (music + ballet) · Evidence: 15 of 26 evidence posts, 46 engagement, 65% brand share-of-voice
Pillar 2 of 3 · Emerging · 31% weight

Real Craft, Real Progression

The play: serious parents struggle to tell classical training from a recital factory; RAD certification (the UK’s foremost dance examining body) and the Vaganova Russian method are verifiable credentials rivals rarely foreground. Viva’s “it’s never too late” also reaches the underserved Adult Returner; rivals skew to early childhood. Insight: “Beauty is born from resilience, not just poise.”

Tension: Beyond the Surface: the true nature of ballet · Evidence: 38 of 1,117 evidence posts, 5,855 engagement, 0% brand share-of-voice
Pillar 3 of 3 · Contested · 31% weight

The Strength Behind the Grace

The play: ballet looks delicate, but parents and students value the strength, discipline and confidence it builds, which is Viva’s own mission, not yet voiced. Insight: “Embrace vulnerability; it is the soil where true strength takes root.”

Tension: Embracing Vulnerability and Strength in Dance · Evidence: 87 of 1,117 evidence posts, 14,783 engagement — highest in the set

Attention already runs hottest in ballet-training access (667 items) and strength & expression (405), rarely in paid, where Viva spends nothing. The plan leans Viva’s voice into all three pillars from there.

Key findings

Three moves for VIVA, ranked by how fast the ground is closing

01

The Strength Behind the Grace: near-absent until 2026, the theme surged to category-leading volume (0→190 per window, verified) as competitors rushed in. Viva is absent, and the window for a distinct angle is closing.

03

FAQ

VIVA case study FAQ

What is a SOMIN brand briefing?
A data-backed content strategy: SOMIN turns brand and competitor content into trends, whitespace, pillars, a calendar and a creative library. Here, Viva against The Ballet School, Singapore Ballet Academy, City Ballet Academy and Cheng Ballet Academy.
What is whitespace analysis in marketing?
It compares the share of its own content a brand and its competitors give each theme, not raw counts, since Viva’s 26 items and competitors’ 1,117 differ 43×. Of five territories, Viva has permission in three: music, classical craft and the strength/confidence reframe.
What does “prior-year verified” mean?
Each trend was checked against the same window a year earlier before being called genuine. Ballet-training access rose year-on-year in 6 of 6 checked periods; the strength surge (0→190, Apr–Jun) held too.
What’s included in a SOMIN-generated content calendar?
Posting slots tied to SOMIN moment triggers from the trend, whitespace and insight data. Viva’s runs 39 posts over 13 weeks in three pillar blocks, anchored to real dates such as National Day.
