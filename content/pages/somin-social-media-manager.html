Questions

What social teams ask first.

Will it post off-brand?
It writes in your account's own voice, and you can keep a human sign-off gate before anything publishes -- approve, edit, or skip each post. Autonomy is a setting, not a default you're stuck with.
How is this different from Buffer or Hootsuite?
Schedulers publish what you give them. SOMIN runs the whole loop -- finds what's landing, writes it in your voice, makes the image, and publishes -- across every channel. See the full comparison with Buffer, Sprout Social & Jasper.
Does it just rewrite trending posts?
No. It grounds each post in what's actually resonating in your category via SOMONITOR, then writes a fresh angle in your voice. Its grounding model retrieves category content more accurately than general-purpose LLMs in a peer-reviewed evaluation.
Which channels does it publish to?
It's built to publish across every channel you run, not a single platform -- the calendar, scheduling, and posting are all part of the loop.
Can I run it fully autonomously?
Yes -- or with a sign-off checkpoint before each post. It already runs SOMIN's own LinkedIn end to end.
