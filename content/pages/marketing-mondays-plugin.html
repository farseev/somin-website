Get the plugin

Set it up · about three minutes

Install the plugin in four steps.

One file brings everything: the 26 skills and the SOMIN connector they read your data through. You need Claude (the desktop app or claude.ai) and a SOMIN account; Figma and Postly are optional.

  1. Sign in: SOMIN first, then Claude

    Sign in at cloud.somin.ai with the account whose workspaces Claude should see, then open claude.ai or the Claude desktop app. Claude links to whichever SOMIN account is signed in when you click Connect, so SOMIN always comes first.

  2. Upload the plugin

    Open Customize › Plugins, click Add › Upload plugin and choose marketing-mondays.plugin from your downloads. It appears under your plugins, switched on. The plugin already holds all 26 skills.

  3. Connect SOMIN from the plugin's page

    Open Marketing Mondays in Customize › Plugins and select its Connectors tab. If SOMIN shows "Not added", click Add, then Connect; if it shows "Not connected", click Connect. On a Team or Enterprise plan, ask your Claude Owner to add the SOMIN connector, then connect it with your own account.

    No Connectors tab? Add SOMIN as a custom connector instead, in Customize › Connectors › Add custom connector:

    Name: SOMINServer URL: https://cloud.somin.ai/_mcp

    Keep the underscore (/_mcp, not /mcp), and use this route instead of the plugin's Connectors tab, not as well.

  4. Check it works, then run your first deck

    Ask Claude which SOMIN workspaces it can see. A list of workspace names within a few seconds means you are connected. Then try the full workflow:

    I have SOMIN MCP connected. Give me a list of workspaces that I can work with, once I choose, give me a list of brands available in my workspace, once I choose the brand, then build a 12-month strategy deck for the one I pick. Use /somin-brand-deck for the full analysis and /somin-brand-guideline for SOMIN styling. Start with /scope-elicitation.

    You can also type / in the message box: the menu lists every skill with the plugin it came from.

Questions

What people ask before they install.

What do I need to use the Marketing Mondays plugin?

Claude (claude.ai or the Claude desktop app) on a plan that lets you add plugins, and a SOMIN account. Figma and Postly are optional and only needed for the make and ship steps.

Is the plugin free?

Yes. The plugin is free to download. Its skills read your data through SOMIN, so they need a SOMIN account to work on your own brand and competitors. Request SOMIN access.

Does it work in Claude Code?

Yes. A plugin added in the Claude app syncs to Claude Code signed in to the same account (run /reload-plugins), or run claude --plugin-dir ./marketing-mondays.zip for one session. Then run /mcp, pick SOMIN and sign in. Skills appear as /marketing-mondays:<skill>.

Where does the data come from?

From your SOMIN workspaces: the competitor posts, ads, studies and audience insights SOMIN already tracks for you. Every number in the outputs traces back to a SOMIN post, study or ID.

What is Marketing Mondays?

A Singapore community of marketers that decodes one real market signal every Monday. SOMIN is its technology partner. The plugin packs the workflow shown live at the session The 2027 marketing team: smaller, faster, or obsolete? on 12 October 2026.
