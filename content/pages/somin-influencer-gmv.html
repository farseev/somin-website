The brief is evidence the manager delivers, in the creator's own voice — it sharpens the human conversation, it doesn't replace it. Frame it as ammunition for the creator, not a stand-in for the relationship.
How is this different from my influencer or affiliate platform?
Those platforms find and pay creators. SOMIN decides what each creator should say — the product territory they can own, the brief in their own voice, and the changes that lift their GMV. It sits on top of your roster, whichever platform sources it. See the full comparison with CreatorIQ, GRIN & Aspire.
Where does the sales data come from?
GMV comes from your programme export today, and the agent reads content and engagement — not the till. The two sources are kept clearly labelled on every output.
Can we hand the briefs straight to creators?
Yes — each brief is presentation-ready and written in the creator's own tone: territory, message angle, concept, and the product it's anchored to. Most teams pass them as-is, or use them as a starting point the creator elevates.
