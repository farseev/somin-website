The Singapore opportunity

Three real mobility problems already present in Singapore

SOMIN did not invent a market: it named three journeys already present in Singapore.

  • ✓ Ageing, active longer — older residents who want to stay mobile and independent: post-op recovery, tired knees, more active years. Mapped to Intelligent Rehabilitation.
  • ✓ An outdoor city — a trail culture on real gradient, from Bukit Timah’s climbs to MacRitchie’s boardwalks and the Southern Ridges. Mapped to For Outdoor Sports.
  • ✓ A walking commute — stair-heavy days across MRT lines and overpasses for professionals who want to arrive without fatigue. Mapped to Smoother Commutes.

The pillars

Three ExoStride applications built for Singapore journeys

One mechanism, reframed for three journeys, each with its own benefits and audience.

Application 1 of 3 · Intelligent Rehabilitation

Intelligent Rehabilitation

Reclaim mobility, confidence and independence. Real-time hip assistance helps you stand, walk and climb again, with up to 80% less lower-body pressure (3–5× less load per step) and gait-reading AI.

Perfect for: Middle-aged & elderly users, people in rehabilitation, individuals with limited mobility. Applications: Post-op recovery walks, regaining stair confidence, staying active for longer, independent daily living.
Application 2 of 3 · For Outdoor Sports

For Outdoor Sports

Go further and explore longer on rugged terrain. Up to 35% less energy on steep climbs and 2–3× walking range per charge, with terrain-intelligent mode switching and IP54 all-weather protection.

Perfect for: Hikers, mountaineers and outdoor-sports enthusiasts. Applications: Steep, powered ascents, rugged uneven trails, all-day treks & range, energy left for the summit.
Application 3 of 3 · Smoother Commutes

Smoother Commutes

Arrive without the fatigue, every single day. 30–50% less commute exertion and up to 4 hours of assistance from a 2-hour charge, with posture detection; 2.4 kg and foldable.

Perfect for: Urban professionals, daily commuters and leisure travellers. Applications: Effortless level ground, stairs & overpasses, all-day power, fold, carry, go.

FAQ

ExoStride case study FAQ

What is ExoStride’s US-H-1.0Pro?
A lightweight, AI-driven powered hip exoskeleton from HT Science that assists in real time, cutting effort, extending range and restoring confidence in rehabilitation, outdoors and on the commute.
What are the three applications this briefing maps ExoStride to in Singapore?
Intelligent Rehabilitation (middle-aged and elderly users, people in rehabilitation, limited mobility), For Outdoor Sports (hikers, mountaineers and outdoor enthusiasts, on four Singapore trail systems) and Smoother Commutes (urban professionals, commuters and leisure travellers).
How does the assistance actually work?
One button starts it, no app needed. It switches modes for level ground, stairs and slopes, starts and pauses as you stand or sit, and reads your gait to deliver up to 16 Nm of torque.
What do the performance numbers, like 80% less lower-body pressure, actually measure?
HT Science’s own measured claims for the US-H-1.0Pro against unassisted movement, not a rival comparison or an independent SOMIN test.
