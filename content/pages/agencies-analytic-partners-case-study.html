FAQ

Analytic Partners case study FAQ

What is a SOMIN cross-market briefing?
A data-backed category read: a year or more of the brand's and competitors' organic and paid content, cross-checked with a practitioner or creator panel and turned into trends, whitespace, pillars and a calendar. Here: 24 months of Analytic Partners, Nielsen, Kantar and Ekimetrics content and a 7-voice panel.
How does SOMIN measure content whitespace against competitors?
It compares a brand's share of its own content per territory with competitors' share of theirs, not raw counts, as cohorts differ in size (here 62, 312 and 36 panel items). Where brand and competitors are quiet but the panel is loud, as with Plan for Volatility (36% vs. 3%), that is genuine whitespace.
What is the 'five layers' SOMIN analysis engine?
Product (brand and competitors), Content (persona to calendar), Trend (windows checked against the prior year), Whitespace (share per cohort) and Insights (the chain that fills the calendar). Here: 21 propositions across three pillars from one persona, three tensions and three stories.
What's included in the briefing's calendar/content recommendations?
A 13-week, 39-post calendar, one fully written lead post per pillar (channel, message, call to action) and a 108-piece creative library across all pillars and funnel stages.
How long does this kind of briefing take to build?
Days rather than weeks: SOMIN typically works 13x faster than manual research. This one covered 24 months, four brands, a panel, three pillars, 39 posts and 108 creative pieces.
