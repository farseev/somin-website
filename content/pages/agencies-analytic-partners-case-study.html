The trend

Business-owner content rose from 7 to 262 items a quarter

Content directed at Business Owners is at its highest in the most recent window — 262 items in 2026 Q2, up from just 7 when SOMIN's 24-month read begins in 2024 Q3. Two forces are driving the surge: Measurement → Decisioning, the category's persistent #1 territory (7→96 items/quarter, sustained and verified against the prior year), and AI-Era Measurement, which barely existed a year ago and is now the largest late-window theme (0→84, verified genuine — the practitioner panel already leads it at 44%).

  • ✓ Read 24 months of Business-Owner-directed content from Analytic Partners, Nielsen, Kantar and Ekimetrics — 12 trend windows, each direction confirmed against the prior year.
  • ✓ The same persona — The Growth-Accountable Marketing Leader — anchors Business-Owner content in all 12 windows: a stable spine to build editorial on.
  • ✓ AI-Era Measurement is a genuine rise, not a blip: effectively absent across the first year of windows, then growing to the largest late-window volume.

FAQ

Analytic Partners case study FAQ

What is a SOMIN cross-market briefing?
A SOMIN cross-market briefing is a data-backed read of a category built from a real analysis of organic and paid content. SOMIN reads a year or more of content from the brand and its named competitors, cross-checks it against a practitioner or creator panel writing for the same audience, then turns the patterns into trend analysis, whitespace mapping, editorial pillars, and a publishing calendar. This example covers 24 months of Business-Owner-directed content from Analytic Partners, Nielsen, Kantar and Ekimetrics, cross-checked against a 7-voice practitioner panel.
How does SOMIN measure content whitespace against competitors?
SOMIN scores each content territory by a brand's share of its own content mix against the same share for its competitors, never raw post counts, since cohorts are rarely the same size. In this briefing, Analytic Partners' 62-item cohort was read against a 312-item competitor cohort and a 36-item practitioner-panel cohort. A territory where the brand and competitors are both quiet but the panel is loud, like Plan for Volatility at 36% of panel content versus 3% of competitor content, is flagged genuine whitespace.
What is the 'five layers' SOMIN analysis engine?
SOMIN's analysis engine runs five layers behind every recommendation: Product (the brand and its named competitors), Content (persona to tensions to angles to stories to propositions to calendar), Trend (multiple trend windows checked against the prior year), Whitespace (brand vs. competitor share within each content cohort), and Insights (the persona-to-proposition chain that fills the calendar). In this briefing, that engine produced 21 propositions across three pillars from a single persona, three tensions and three stories.
What's included in the briefing's calendar/content recommendations?
The briefing includes a 13-week, 39-post publishing calendar organized into contiguous pillar blocks, plus one fully-written lead post per pillar covering channel, message and call to action, and a 108-piece creative library spanning all three pillars across Awareness, Consideration and Conversion funnel stages.
How long does this kind of briefing take to build?
This briefing — 24 months of category research across four brands and a practitioner panel, three whitespace-backed editorial pillars, a 13-week/39-post calendar, and a 108-piece creative library — is the kind of work SOMIN's platform typically completes 13x faster than manual research workflows.
