How it works

From a live campaign to a fresh, scored funnel.

It runs a few days into a live campaign — enough delivery data to read what's working, before fatigue sets in.

01
Read the live campaign

It reads which ads are actually winning for your goal — awareness, traffic, leads, and sales each change what "good" means — and flags what's fatiguing or too alike.

02
Decode the winners

It works out the concept behind the winning ads, and where the category's paid whitespace sits, from SOMONITOR.

03
Generate a fresh funnel

It builds a new set of on-brand ads across the funnel — top, middle, and bottom — in that open space.

04
Score before spend

Every new ad is scored for likely click-through before a penny is spent, and the strongest are flagged.

05
Push, paused

The top-scoring ads go into the same campaign — mirroring the winner's landing page, copy, and CTA — built paused until you approve the spend.

06
Approve the spend

Nothing activates without your sign-off. Predictions are shown as predictions, and the approval gate before spend is non-negotiable.

07
Learn from results

It compares each prediction against the real result and feeds that back — so the next round is sharper.

Questions

What performance teams ask first.

Can a model really beat my media buyer's instinct?
It doesn't sideline the buyer. It clears the obvious losers so their judgment goes on the close calls — and every score is shown as a prediction, closed with a real-results check once the ad runs.
Does it spend money without my approval?
No. The top-scoring ads are pushed into your campaign built paused, mirroring the winning ad's page, copy, and CTA. Nothing activates until you approve the spend.
How is this different from AdCreative.ai or Smartly.io?
Those optimise the delivery of, or spin variations on, creative you already have. SOMIN decides what to make from what's actually winning in your category, and scores whether it will work before you spend. See the full comparison with AdCreative.ai, Smartly.io & Meta Advantage+.
What if I have no paid data yet — a cold start?
It scores the creative itself against live category signal to decide what to run, then re-scores every time from the campaign's actual performance once ads go live.
What predicts the score?
SOMIN's in-house SoCraft models, trained on tens of thousands of labelled ads and published at ACM WSDM 2023 — 78% CTR-prediction accuracy, about 3× the human baseline.
