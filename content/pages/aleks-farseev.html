Prof. Aleks Farseev (also written Aleksandr Farseev, 法信夫) is the founder and CEO of SOMIN (SoMin.ai), the AI marketing-intelligence company run by Somin Pte. Ltd. in Singapore. He is a computer scientist who holds a Ph.D. from the National University of Singapore, teaches and researches AI for marketing, and writes for the Forbes Technology Council. This page collects the facts he publishes on farseev.com.

Who is Prof. Aleks Farseev?

He is a researcher turned founder. He started in social-media AI research, then built SOMIN to bring that research to advertisers, agencies and creators. He works between Singapore, London and Hong Kong, and also performs as a professional accordionist.

Current roles

  • Founder and CEO of SOMIN, the platform behind SOMONITOR.
  • Fractional CTO and board member at DEFY Group.
  • Guest professor at the National University of Singapore (NUS) and INSEAD; research professor at ITMO University; faculty at the Singapore University of Social Sciences (SUSS).
  • Columnist and Marketing and Advertising community leader at the Forbes Technology Council.
  • Founder of Accordion Singapore; APAC and Southeast Asia ambassador for Faith Driven Entrepreneur; co-founder of Faith Driven Singapore.

Education and research

He earned a Ph.D. in Computer Science (social-media AI) at the NUS School of Computing, 2013–2017, with a focus on multi-view user profiling. His research areas include:

  • multi-view user profiling and cross-network recommendation;
  • multimodal sensor fusion, linking digital signals to physical wellness;
  • generative AI explainability and reducing hallucination;
  • machine learning, large language models, agentic AI and AdTech.

His site lists more than 30 peer-reviewed papers at venues such as ACM Multimedia, The Web Conference, SIGIR and WSDM. Selected titles include Against Opacity: Explainable AI and LLMs for Effective Digital Advertising (ACM Multimedia 2023), Personality-Driven Social Multimedia Content Recommendation (ACM Multimedia 2022), Cross-Domain Recommendation via Clustering on Multi-Layer Graphs (SIGIR 2017) and the SOMIN platform papers at WSDM 2021 and ACM Multimedia 2018. The site also notes one US patent application on automated insight generation with large language models; it does not state that the patent has been granted. Browse the work on the research page.

Recognition

As listed on his own site: Gartner Cool Vendor in AI for Marketing (2020), Echelon Top 10 Startups from e27 (2025, MarTech and GenAI), the SBR National Business Award for Computer Software (2022), the Web Science Trust Person of the Year award for social-media user profiling, the NUS VaSCo Award and the Dean's Research Excellence Award (2017).

Speaking and writing

He gives keynotes on generative AI, marketing and advertising. His site names invited talks at NUS, NTU, INSEAD and Bournemouth University, an industry keynote at ACM Multimedia 2025 and a keynote at DMCKL 2025 in Kuala Lumpur. His Forbes Technology Council columns include Redemptive Marketing. Press profiles include Salt&Light on how faith shaped the founding of SOMIN.

Faith and community

Prof. Farseev is an Orthodox Christian. He co-founded Faith Driven Singapore, the Singapore chapter of Faith Driven Entrepreneur, and he links his work to what SOMIN calls redemptive marketing. Read the SOMIN vision for how this shapes the product.

Music

He is a professional accordionist and the founder of Accordion Singapore, a community that promotes accordion culture and education.
