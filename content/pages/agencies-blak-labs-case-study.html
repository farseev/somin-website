The client

Blak Labs, a 50-person Singapore agency built on structure

Blak Labs is a Singapore advertising and communications agency with about 50 employees and around S$5 million in annual revenue. It chose tooling over headcount to match rivals' larger research benches.

The challenge

Pitching against bigger benches without a bigger research budget

Blak Labs needed agency-grade insight for each pitch, including speculative ones, without days of manual research.

  • ✓ Rivals had deeper research and planning benches.
  • ✓ Manual brand analysis could take days per pitch.
  • ✓ Content audits and client reporting took several FTEs without proportionate output.
  • ✓ Much of the salary cost went to repetitive manual work.
The approach

Four SOMIN modules across Blak Labs' pitching and reporting

Four connected modules replaced Blak Labs' manual pitch research and content audits.

01

Brand Tracker

On-demand, real-time competitive intelligence across social media.

02

Perspective Studies

Maps crowded messaging and gaps into a proprietary category audit per pitch.

03

Expressions

Turns the insight into market-ready creative ideas for pitches and campaigns.

04

Content Library

Automates content audits, freeing the 0.5 FTE they used to take.

The outcome

Brand analysis from 10 hours to 1, reporting cost $10K to $6K

Research, planning and reporting all became faster and cheaper, and strategy teams now enter each pitch with the competitive set understood.

Understanding competitive sets is very valuable. What took days of research before now takes hours. SOMIN has streamlined our pitching process, given us quicker insights into activities by competitive brands across social media, and enabled us to identify gaps in the market to develop new strategies for our clients.
