Recommendations

Four moves to grow Daisy's reach

Cadence, mix and lane changes, each grounded in a SOMIN engagement figure.

Move 1 of 4 · Engagement ↑ · Weeks 1–2

Lead with the tender-cozy register

Cozy-connection posts earn ~10,400 engagements each vs. ~3,900 for style/fashion, about 2.7×. Lead with warm, dog's-POV moments at home.

Move 2 of 4 · Reach ↑ · Plan now, push Oct–Dec

Concentrate belonging content into the festive window

The category's cozy-connection backbone grows from 10 posts in summer to 44 in Oct–Dec, about 4.4×. Front-load her strongest “you belong here” content there.

Move 3 of 4 · Distinctiveness · Ongoing discipline

Don't chase the pet-humor lane head-on

pesik_mishka owns pet humor: about 55,000 engagements per post and 52% of competitor share of voice. Humor stays seasoning in Daisy's dog's-POV voice.

Move 4 of 4 · New reach ↑ · Month 2–3

Occupy the cozy-home “you belong here” lane

Cozy home is 16% of her content vs. 3% for rivals, yet only about 5 posts per 90 days. A repeatable “welcome home / saving your spot” format would claim it.
