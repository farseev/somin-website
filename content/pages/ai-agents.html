Questions

What teams ask about the agents.

What is an "AI agent" in SOMIN?
Each agent owns a marketing job end to end — deciding the angle, scoring the ad, briefing the creator, running the email test, posting daily, or reporting it back. They're not chat assistants: they read live category signal via SOMONITOR, do the work, and hand you an output ready to act on — a deck, a funnel, a brief, a sequence, a dashboard.
Which agents are live, and which are coming?
Strategy, Paid Media, Influencer & GMV, and the Social Media Manager are live today. The CRM & Email agent is in preview — the engine runs, the guided approval flow is rolling out. Social Performance Reporting is coming, built on reporting-time gains customers already see. Talk to us about early access to the preview and upcoming agents.
Do the agents act without my approval?
No. Agentic doesn't mean unattended. Paid Media pushes ads built paused until you approve the spend; the CRM agent needs two approvals and stops in under a minute; the Social agent runs autonomously only if you choose — otherwise it waits at a sign-off checkpoint. Predictions are always shown as predictions.
What do all the agents have in common?
SOMONITOR, SOMIN's real-time category engine. Every agent reasons from what's actually winning in your category right now, refreshed live — not a generic model, a mention feed, or a quarterly survey. One engine underneath; six jobs on top.
How is this different from ChatGPT or a general AI assistant?
General assistants answer from the open web. SOMIN's agents answer from the creative actually winning in your category, score concepts before you spend, and output work in your brand. See the full breakdown on the Strategy comparison vs ChatGPT, Perplexity, Meltwater, and GWI.
What models and infrastructure power the agents?
Google Cloud, with Gemini 2.5 Pro for large-scale ad and video understanding and idea generation, alongside SOMIN's in-house SoCraft CTR models — published at ACM WSDM 2023, 78% CTR-prediction accuracy, about 3× the human baseline. An explainable-AI layer keeps every recommendation traceable to the underlying data.
Is the research peer-reviewed?
Yes — the platform and its models are published at top-tier ACM venues, including ACM Multimedia 2025, ACM Multimedia 2023, and ACM WSDM 2023, with the research line going back to ACM WSDM 2021 and ACM Multimedia 2018.
How fast can I see an agent working?
Book a 30-minute walkthrough on your own category — median 13 minutes from a brief to a stack of pre-scored concepts, independently documented in Google Cloud's customer story on SOMIN.
