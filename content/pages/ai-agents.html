    Questions

    What teams ask about the agents.

    What is an "AI agent" in SOMIN?
    An agent owns one marketing job end to end: it reads live signal via SOMONITOR, does the work and delivers a deck, funnel, brief, sequence or dashboard.
    Which agents are live, and which are coming?
    Strategy, Paid Media, Influencer & GMV and Social Media Manager are live; CRM & Email is in preview and Social Performance Reporting is coming.
    Do the agents act without my approval?
    No. Paid Media ads wait paused for approval, CRM needs two approvals and stops within a minute, and Social runs autonomously only by choice. Predictions are labelled as such.
    What do all the agents have in common?
    SOMONITOR, SOMIN's real-time category engine — not a generic model, mention feed or quarterly survey.
    How is this different from ChatGPT or a general AI assistant?
    General assistants answer from the open web; SOMIN's agents work from your category, pre-score concepts and produce on-brand work. See the Strategy comparison vs ChatGPT, Perplexity, Meltwater and GWI.
    What models and infrastructure power the agents?
    Google Cloud: Gemini 2.5 Pro for ad and video understanding and idea generation, SOMIN's SoCraft CTR models (ACM WSDM 2023; 78% accuracy, about 3× the human baseline) and an explainable-AI layer for traceability.
    Is the research peer-reviewed?
    Yes, at ACM venues including ACM Multimedia 2025, ACM Multimedia 2023 and ACM WSDM 2023; the research goes back to ACM WSDM 2021 and ACM Multimedia 2018.
    How fast can I see an agent working?
    In a 30-minute walkthrough: a median 13 minutes from brief to pre-scored concepts, as documented in Google Cloud's customer story on SOMIN.
