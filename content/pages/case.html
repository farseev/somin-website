Miraflora Wagyu × GWI

How SOMONITOR used Global Web Index (GWI) consumer analytics to uncover the personas, content preferences and behaviour patterns behind Miraflora's premium beef positioning.

Get the full case study Book a call

The challenge

Demographics weren't enough.

Miraflora, a premium beef eCommerce brand in the USA, partnered with SOMONITOR to refine its marketing strategy with consumer insights from the Global Web Index (GWI). Traditional demographic data wasn't enough to explain who buys premium wagyu online, or why.

The approach

GWI analytics, read by SOMONITOR.

  • Uncovered the key audience personas behind Miraflora's premium positioning.
  • Mapped the content preferences of each persona.
  • Identified the behavioural patterns that shape purchase decisions.

The outcome

Precision targeting instead of guesswork.

The data-driven approach let Miraflora reach the right consumers with precision.
