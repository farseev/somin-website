Common questions

“We already have AI creative.”

Most brands do. What is missing is knowing which to run.

How is this different from a generative creative tool?
They produce options. SOMIN decides what those say and whether they beat the category baseline; most tools cover only production, one of four steps.
Will it stay on brand?
Yes. Frames use your Figma components, licensed photography and a composited wordmark, never a generated identity; a locked prompt suffix fixes palette, type and copy density.
Do we have to give you our Figma file?
No. It is optional; without it you get the strategy, propositions and scored expressions and build the final frames yourself.
What does “scored” actually mean?
Each creative gets a predicted clickability class against the category corpus and a confidence figure. It passes only if the class is above average and confidence reaches 0.80 — a check most pre-flight scoring skips.
How fast does a team adopt it?
There is nothing to learn or prepare: name the brand, moment and market, and the first whitespace read returns in minutes.
