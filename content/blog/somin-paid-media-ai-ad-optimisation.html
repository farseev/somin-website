SOMIN Paid Media is the paid-advertising solution from SOMIN (SoMin.ai), the AI marketing-intelligence company run by Somin Pte. Ltd. in Singapore. It reads which ads are winning in your category, builds a fresh on-brand funnel in the open space and scores each ad for likely click-through before any media spend. Winning ads are pushed into your campaign paused, until you approve the spend.

Key takeaways

  • SOMIN Paid Media decides what to make and run. It does not just optimise delivery of creative you already have.
  • Every ad is scored against SOMONITOR, SOMIN's real-time category engine, for likely click-through before spend.
  • SOMIN reports 78% CTR-prediction accuracy from its SoCraft models, peer-reviewed at ACM WSDM 2023.
  • Nothing activates without your sign-off: top ads are built paused, and the approval gate before spend is non-negotiable.
  • SOMIN says it is often used together with delivery tools such as Meta Advantage+ or Smartly.io.

What is SOMIN Paid Media?

It is a SOMIN solution, status Live, that plans and scores paid creative. The SOMIN Paid Media page sets it against tools that "optimise what you made": SOMIN's job is to decide what to make from what is winning in your category now.

It is built for the team that owns media efficiency: the Head of Performance or Agency MD, the paid or performance lead, and the creative, account and finance colleagues who depend on cleaner spend.

How does it plan and score ads, step by step?

SOMIN says it runs a few days into a live campaign, with enough delivery data to read what is working before fatigue sets in. It then follows seven steps:

  1. Read the live campaign. It reads which ads win for your goal (awareness, traffic, leads or sales each change what "good" means) and flags what is fatiguing or too alike.
  2. Decode the winners. It works out the concept behind the winning ads and where the category's paid whitespace sits, using SOMONITOR.
  3. Generate a fresh funnel. It builds new on-brand ads across the top, middle and bottom of the funnel, in that open space.
  4. Score before spend. Each new ad is scored for likely click-through, and the strongest are flagged.
  5. Push, paused. Top ads go into the same campaign, mirroring the winner's landing page, copy and CTA, built paused.
  6. Approve the spend. Nothing activates without your sign-off. Predictions are shown as predictions.
  7. Learn from results. It compares each prediction with the real result and feeds that back into the next round.

For the idea behind pre-scoring, read predicting ad performance before you spend and what creative clickability is. The whitespace step is explained in what marketing whitespace is.

How accurate is the scoring?

SOMIN states 78% CTR-prediction accuracy across customer benchmarks, about three times the human baseline. It attributes the score to its in-house SoCraft deep-learning models, published at ACM WSDM 2023 and run end to end on live Meta accounts.

Read this with care. The 78% and the 3x baseline are SOMIN's own published figures, and the product page does not define the accuracy test or the baseline in detail. Ask for the paper and for a trial on your own category before you treat it as a promise.

The SOMIN pages also give two figures for the training set: "tens of thousands" of labelled ads in the proof text and "10k+" in a stat tile. We therefore say only that the models were trained on labelled ads.

Does it spend money without approval?

No, per SOMIN. The top-scoring ads are pushed into your campaign built paused, and nothing activates until you approve the spend. SOMIN also says the model does not sideline the media buyer. It clears the obvious losers, so the buyer's judgment goes on the close calls.

What if there is no paid data yet?

SOMIN says it can work cold or live. With no ad data, it scores the creative itself against live category signal to decide what to run. Once ads go live, it re-scores from the campaign's real performance.
