The big APAC shopping dates in 2027 are 6.18 on Friday 18 June, 9.9 on Thursday 9 September, 10.10 on Sunday 10 October, 11.11 on Thursday 11 November, 12.12 on Sunday 12 December and Black Friday on Friday 26 November. Each belongs to different markets and platforms, and the double dates are fixed to the calendar, so the weekday changes every year.

Key takeaways

  • Double-date sales fall on the same date every year; only Black Friday moves, because it follows US Thanksgiving.
  • China's 6.18 and 11.11 anchor the year, and the sale often runs for weeks, not a single day.
  • In Southeast Asia, 9.9, 11.11 and 12.12 matter; peak days differ by country.
  • Indonesia has its own 12.12 event, and India's 10.10 belongs to a different platform story.
  • Platform names and promotion windows change, so confirm them with each platform before you commit budget.

How does the season build through the year?

It builds in steps: 6.18 mid-year, then a run of double dates from September to December. The second half starts with 9.9 and ends with 12.12.

Criteo's double-dates digest reported regional spikes across Southeast Asia of 338% for 9/9, 399% for 11/11 and 453% for 12/12 in its 2022 data. Those figures describe that year's retail sales, not a forecast for 2027, but they show why all three dates deserve a plan.

Which dates matter in which market?

Lead with the dates local data supports, not the full list.

  • China: 6.18 and 11.11. JD.com explains 618 as its anniversary sale; Alibaba and JD.com generated US$115 billion together on 11.11 in 2020, per dLocal.
  • Southeast Asia: 9.9, 11.11 and 12.12, with Black Friday as the final peak. Criteo found most countries peaked on 11.11, while Vietnam and Indonesia peaked on 12.12.
  • Indonesia: 12.12 has its own local event, HarBolNas, which dLocal calls the country's biggest annual ecommerce shopping event.
  • India: Flipkart's Big Billion Days Sale is listed against 10.10, though India's festive timing is driven mostly by Diwali, which falls on 28 or 29 October 2027 (see our APAC and MENA marketing calendar).
  • Greater China readers searching for a 2027 marketing events calendar (2027行銷行事曆) will find the same fixed dates plus local holidays.

How should you plan the 2027 shopping calendar?

Pick the three or four dates that matter for each market, then plan backwards. SOMIN's working rule for double-date sales is to start planning 8 to 10 weeks ahead and go live one to two weeks before, with teasers on 9.9 and 10.10. That is a guideline, not an industry average.

  1. Mark fixed dates: 18 June, 9 September, 10 October, 11 November and 12 December.
  2. Add Black Friday (26 November) and Cyber Monday (29 November).
  3. For each market, drop the dates your category data does not support.
  4. Give each date a different job: teaser, peak, last chance.
  5. Keep one week after each peak for follow-up and returns.

FAQ

What day is 11.11 in 2027?

Thursday 11 November 2027. Singles' Day is on 11 November every year.

When is Black Friday 2027 in APAC?

Friday 26 November 2027, the day after US Thanksgiving on 25 November. In the Gulf it is often called White Friday.

What is the biggest shopping event in Southeast Asia?

It depends on the country. Criteo found most countries peaked on 11.11, while Vietnam and Indonesia peaked on 12.12.

What is 6.18?

It is a mid-year shopping festival in China, named after JD.com's anniversary on 18 June. JD says it has run about 20 days since 2014.

Is 9.9 a Shopee event?

Shopee launched 9.9 Super Shopping Day in 2016, according to dLocal, and it is now a wider Southeast Asian shopping date.
