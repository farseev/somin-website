Japan has 16 national holidays in 2027. The biggest planning window is Golden Week, from Thursday 29 April to Wednesday 5 May, with three holidays in a row from 3 to 5 May. Other peaks for brands are the New Year, Valentine's Day and White Day, the start of the business and school year in April, summer Obon, Black Friday and the year-end gift season.

Key takeaways

  • Golden Week 2027 runs from 29 April to 5 May; 30 April is a working day, so many people take it off.
  • Vernal Equinox Day falls on a Sunday, so Monday 22 March is the substitute holiday.
  • There is no Silver Week in September 2027, because the holidays on 20 and 23 September are not joined.
  • Obon in mid-August is not a public holiday, but many people travel.
  • April is the start of the fiscal and school year, a key moment for new routines and purchases.

How should you plan around Golden Week 2027?

Golden Week starts with Showa Day on Thursday 29 April. Friday 30 April is a working day, then the weekend and three holidays run from Saturday 1 May to Wednesday 5 May. People who take 30 April off get a seven-day break. Travel, leisure, outdoor and food brands peak before and during the break; many B2B audiences are hard to reach that week. Run travel and booking ads from March, and shift B2B campaigns to the weeks around it.

Which shopping peaks matter beyond the holidays?

  • New Year: lucky bags (fukubukuro) and first sales in early January.
  • Valentine's Day, 14 February, and White Day, 14 March: chocolate and gifts, with the return gift a month later.
  • April: the new fiscal and school year, with new employees, students and moves.
  • Summer: the mid-year gift season and Obon travel in mid-August.
  • Black Friday, 26 November: now common among Japanese retailers; see Black Friday 2027 by market.
  • December: year-end gifts, Christmas and the year-end break, when many companies close from about 29 December.

FAQ

When is Golden Week 2027 in Japan?

From Thursday 29 April to Wednesday 5 May 2027. Friday 30 April is a working day; the holidays on 3, 4 and 5 May follow a weekend.

Is there a Silver Week in 2027?

No. Respect for the Aged Day is on Monday 20 September and Autumnal Equinox Day on Thursday 23 September, with working days in between.

Is Obon a public holiday?

No, but many people take time off around 13 to 15 August to visit family, so travel demand peaks.

How many national holidays does Japan have in 2027?

Sixteen, including the substitute holiday for Vernal Equinox Day on Monday 22 March.
