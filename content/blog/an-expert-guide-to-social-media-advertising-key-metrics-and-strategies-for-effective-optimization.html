The metrics that matter most in social media advertising follow the funnel. Click-through rate (CTR) shows whether the ad and the audience fit, the ratio of clicks to landing page views shows whether those clicks reach your page, and conversion rate and cost per conversion show whether the traffic pays. Where conversions are confirmed in a CRM rather than on the platform, as in banking, you estimate cost per conversion from CRM data.

Key takeaways

  • A falling CTR usually signals ad fatigue or less appealing content; refresh the creative, add an exclusive offer or revise the targeting, and watch frequency.
  • A click-to-landing-page-view ratio of about 80% is a healthy mark; lower figures point to slow pages, tracking issues or audience mismatches.
  • Optimise directly for the conversion event when it happens often, and for an intermediate event such as add to cart when it does not.
  • In regulated industries, conversions may only be visible in the CRM, so funnel ratios from the CRM decide profitability.
  • Offline conversion data and conversion APIs improve optimisation, but they need technical expertise to set up.

What are social media ads for?

This guide covers social media advertising on platforms like Meta and TikTok. In our experience, social media ads, including banners and programmatic ads, mainly serve to engage new audiences by aligning with their immediate interests. That alignment shapes how campaign managers measure success, through different metrics at each stage of the marketing funnel.

The difference from search matters. On search, people arrive with a question; on social, they are scrolling and your ad interrupts them. So the first job of a social ad is to earn attention, and the metrics follow the path from attention to action.

How do you read click-through rate?

CTR is clicks divided by impressions, and it gauges how compelling your ads are. A high CTR indicates effective creatives and audience targeting, leading to both traffic and conversions. A drop in CTR may signal ad fatigue or less appealing content, which calls for refreshed creatives, exclusive offers or revised audience targeting.

Monitoring ad frequency is also essential to avoid overexposure. Frequency is impressions divided by reach: the average number of times each person has seen the ad. When frequency climbs while CTR falls, the same people are seeing the same ad too often.

What is a good click-to-landing-page-view ratio?

The step from clicks to landing page views is critical. In our experience, an ideal click-to-view ratio is about 80%, which shows that people who click are actually reaching and loading the page. Low views can result from slow loading, tracking issues or audience mismatches.

Comparing "link clicks" with "total clicks" also helps you understand engagement. On Meta, total clicks include any click on the ad, such as reactions, profile clicks or expanding the text, while link clicks count only clicks to your destination. A large gap means people are interacting with the ad without going further. Landing page views are only counted when the page loads with the pixel working, so a sudden fall can be a tracking fault rather than a change in behaviour.

Which conversion metrics should you optimise for?

The ultimate goal in performance marketing is conversion, whether that is lead generation or sales. For frequent conversions, you can optimise directly for the conversion event. For less frequent events, intermediate metrics such as "add to cart" become important.

The reason is learning. Delivery systems need a steady flow of the event you optimise for to work out who is likely to convert. If purchases are rare, optimising for a more common step earlier in the funnel gives the system enough signal, and you can move to the final event once volume allows.

Conversion rates reflect the relevance of your product and the effectiveness of your landing page. Read them alongside cost per conversion (spend divided by conversions) and, where you track revenue, return on ad spend (revenue divided by spend).

How do you track conversions through a CRM?

In industries like banking, tracking conversions may depend on internal CRM systems because of privacy concerns. Understanding your funnel's conversion ratios is crucial for effective campaign management: for example, how many leads become qualified applications, and how many applications are approved.

Even without direct tracking on the platforms, estimating the cost per conversion from CRM data is vital for profitability. Divide the spend behind a set of leads by the number of those leads the CRM confirms as converted, and you have a real cost per conversion to set against the platform's cost per lead.

How can you improve targeting and attribution?

Use data for audience targeting, but be aware that each platform limits how much of your audience you can reach and see. Offline conversion data and conversion APIs, such as Meta's Conversions API and TikTok's Events API, send conversion events from your server or CRM straight to the platform in near real time. They make optimisation more accurate, though they require technical expertise and extensive revisions to set up.

Accurate attribution in your CRM and detailed tracking of conversion sources can significantly improve ad set optimisation. When the platform learns which clicks became real customers, not just leads, it can find more people like them.

FAQ

What does a falling click-through rate mean?

It usually signals ad fatigue or content that appeals less to the audience. Refresh the creative, add an exclusive offer or revise the targeting, and check whether frequency is climbing.

What is a good click-to-landing-page-view ratio?

About 80% is a healthy mark in our experience. Lower ratios often come from slow pages, tracking problems or an audience that does not match the page.

Should I optimise for purchases or for add to cart?

Optimise for the conversion itself when it happens often. When it is infrequent, optimise for an intermediate event such as add to cart so the platform has enough data to learn from.

How do I measure conversions that only show up in my CRM?

Work out your funnel's conversion ratios in the CRM and estimate cost per conversion from them. Offline conversion uploads or a conversion API can then feed those results back to the ad platform.
