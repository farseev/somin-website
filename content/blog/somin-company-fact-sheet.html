What is SOMIN?

SOMIN is an AI marketing-intelligence company. Its SOMONITOR platform turns verified listening data on competitor ads, posts and creators into insights, briefs, pre-scored campaign concepts and on-brand content ideas.

SOMIN's own tagline is "Stop guessing what works. Start knowing." The software listens to a category: every competitor ad, post and creator on Meta, Google, TikTok, LinkedIn and YouTube. Its AI agents follow a fixed research process and trace each output back to its source. A typical brief becomes scored ideas in about 13 minutes, according to SOMIN's product description.

The industry label that fits best is marketing technology, and more precisely competitor monitoring and campaign planning. The product sits between social listening, ad intelligence and creative planning. For a plain-language comparison, see social listening vs audience research vs ad optimisation.

What is the company's name, and what is the difference between SOMIN and SOMONITOR?

The company is SOMIN, legally Somin Pte. Ltd. SOMONITOR is the name of its platform. You will also see SoMin, SoMin.ai and Somin.AI, which all refer to the same company.

Use "SOMIN" in capitals when you write about the company, and use product names as they appear on the product pages.

Who is SOMIN for, and what does it include?

SOMIN serves three audiences: advertising and media agencies, brand marketing teams, and creator and affiliate programs. Each has its own page:

  • SOMIN for agencies: pitch, reporting and strategy support, plus white-label deployment.
  • SOMIN for brands: competitor intelligence and campaign planning for in-house teams.
  • SOMIN for creators: creator selection and sales-focused creator briefs.

The platform's listed features are:

  • Brand Tracker: daily competitor ad and content intelligence across Meta, Google, TikTok, LinkedIn and YouTube.
  • Perspective Studies: persona, tension, proposition and white-space analysis.
  • Expressions: AI-generated campaign concepts, briefs and on-brand visuals.
  • Ad and content scoring: predictive clickability before launch.
  • Agentic research process with traceable sources.
  • Connections to Claude, Manus and Codex (MCP) and to Zapier.
