The Biblioteca de anuncios de Meta is the Spanish name for the Meta Ad Library, Meta's public archive of ads running on its apps. To analyse competitors in Spain and Latin America, search one country at a time, find each rival's Page, record every active ad with the date, and tag the ads with one shared codebook. It shows what rivals say, not how well their ads perform.

Key takeaways

  • "Biblioteca de anuncios" is the Spanish name of the Meta Ad Library; Brazilian teams search for "Biblioteca de Anúncios".
  • Run each country separately: Spain, Mexico, Colombia, Argentina and Chile differ in offers and wording.
  • Search by the advertiser's Page, not only by keyword.
  • Ads delivered in Spain fall under EU rules, so they carry more detail than ads seen only in Latin America.
  • Capture ads while they run, with a screenshot and a date.

What is the Biblioteca de anuncios de Meta?

It is the same tool as the Meta Ad Library, shown in Spanish. In 2019 Meta said the library would show "all active ads any Page is running" and could be searched by Page as well as by keyword (Meta, 2019). Searches such as biblioteca de anuncios meta, biblioteca meta or meta anuncios biblioteca all lead to it.

How do you search it country by country?

  1. Open the library and choose the country first. Do not mix Spain and Mexico in one search; their offers and language differ.
  2. Keep the category on all ads, unless you study political or social-issue ads.
  3. Type the brand name and pick the Page from the suggestions. A keyword search also returns other advertisers whose ads contain the word.
  4. Filter by status, platform or language if the interface offers it.
  5. Open each ad and note the start date, status and format.
  6. Save the link, ad ID, screenshot and capture date.

Repeat the same searches each week or month, so changes are easy to see. Our guide to researching competitor ads in the Meta Ad Library includes a recording sheet you can reuse.

What is different for Spain?

Spain is in the EU, so the Digital Services Act applies. Meta said in 2023 that ads targeting people in the EU would be stored in the library for a year, with information on age, gender and location targeting and the dates they ran (Meta, 2023). That makes Spain the easiest market to study over time. For Latin American countries we found no equivalent statement, so treat the library there as a view of what is active now.

What should you record for each ad?

FieldWhy it matters
OfferDiscount, instalments (meses sin intereses in Mexico, cuotas in Argentina), free shipping, free trial
Language and toneTú or usted, local slang, neutral Spanish for several markets
FormatVideo, image, carousel, creator content
Call to actionBuy, sign up, send a message (WhatsApp is common in the region)
Start dateHow long a rival keeps an idea running

Write the tags in one language and keep the original Spanish copy next to them. Remove near-identical variations before counting, or one heavily tested ad will look like a trend.

What can you not see?

For ordinary commercial ads you should not expect spend, reach or results. The library tells you which messages a rival is paying for, not which ones work. Long-running ads are a hint, not proof, that an ad performs.

How do you turn the findings into a plan?

Count the patterns per country: which offers, tones and formats dominate, and which are missing. The gaps are your options. FAQ

Is the Biblioteca de anuncios free?

Yes. Anyone can search it without an ad account. You need a Facebook login only for some extra tools.

Can I see how much a competitor spends in Mexico or Spain?

Not for ordinary commercial ads. Spend and reach ranges are shown for political and social-issue ads. For ads targeting the EU, Meta adds targeting and delivery details, but not spend.

Why do I see different ads for the same brand in each country?

Brands often run separate Pages or campaigns per market. Choose the country first and check which Page runs the ads there.

How often should I check the library?

Weekly in fast categories such as retail and fintech, monthly in slower ones. Keep the same searches each time.
