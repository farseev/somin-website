Generative engine optimisation (GEO) is the work of making your brand appear, and appear correctly, in answers from AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Perplexity. It builds on SEO: clear pages, consistent facts and trusted mentions. The difference is that AI systems summarise and cite, so you need content that answers questions directly and facts that agree everywhere.

Key takeaways

  • Answer real questions in the first lines of a page, in plain language.
  • Keep company facts identical across your site, profiles and directories.
  • Use clear structure: headings as questions, short paragraphs, tables, FAQs and structured data.
  • Earn mentions on sites that AI systems cite, not just links.
  • Monitor what AI assistants say about you, and correct the sources behind wrong answers.

How is GEO different from SEO?

SEO aims for a ranking on a results page. GEO aims for inclusion in an answer. AI assistants with web search find pages, pull out passages and write a summary, often with citations. Pages that state a clear answer, with facts that are easy to extract and confirmed elsewhere, are easier to use. Our essay on the end of SEO and the agentic web explains the wider shift.

What is on the GEO checklist?

Content

  • Start each page with a one-paragraph answer to the main question.
  • Write headings as the questions people ask, in their words and languages.
  • Give specific facts: numbers, dates, names, prices, with a source.
  • Add a short FAQ with complete, standalone answers.

Facts and entities

  • Use the same company name, description, founders, locations and product names everywhere.
  • Keep an "about" page and a press page with the canonical facts.
  • Update profiles on LinkedIn, review sites and directories when facts change.

Structure and access

  • Add structured data (schema.org) for your organisation, products, articles and FAQs.
  • Make sure pages render as text without scripts, load fast and are not blocked for the crawlers you want.
  • Consider an llms.txt file that points AI systems to your key pages.

Mentions

  • Get covered by trusted publications, research, reviews and industry lists in your category.
  • Publish original data that others will quote.

How do you measure AI visibility?

Write a fixed list of prompts that real buyers would ask, such as "best social listening tool for agencies in Southeast Asia". Run them in several assistants every month and record whether your brand appears, how it is described, which competitors appear and which sources are cited. Compare the cited sources with your own pages to find what is missing.

What do you do when an AI answer is wrong?

Find where the wrong fact comes from: an old page, a directory or an article. Fix your own pages first, then ask the other sites to update. Make the correct fact easy to find on your site, in text and in structured data. Expect changes to take weeks, because AI systems refresh their sources at different speeds.

Where does competitor research fit?

AI answers often compare brands. Knowing how competitors describe themselves, and which claims they repeat, shows where your positioning is unclear or crowded. SOMIN Strategy maps competitor messages across channels, which helps you choose claims that are both true and distinct.

FAQ

What is GEO in marketing?

Generative engine optimisation: making content and facts easy for AI assistants to find, trust and cite, so your brand appears correctly in their answers.

Does GEO replace SEO?

No. AI assistants with web search still rely on pages that search engines can crawl. GEO adds answer-first writing, consistent facts and monitoring of AI answers.

How long does GEO take to work?

Weeks to months. Your pages need to be crawled, and AI systems refresh what they use at different speeds.

Can AI assistants make mistakes about my brand?

Yes. They can repeat outdated or wrong facts. Check regularly and fix the sources behind wrong answers.
