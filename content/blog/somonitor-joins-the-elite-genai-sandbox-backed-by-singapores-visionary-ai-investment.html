In 2024, the Singapore government included SOMIN's SOMONITOR platform in the first batch of its GenAI Sandbox, a programme run by Enterprise Singapore and IMDA that lets businesses trial generative AI solutions with government support. The move came as Budget 2024 committed more than S$1 billion over five years to AI compute, talent and industry development under the National AI Strategy 2.0.

What did Singapore's Budget 2024 commit to AI?

Singapore has long been a frontrunner in using technology for progress. The 2024 Budget outlines more than S$1 billion of investment to support the National AI Strategy 2.0 and an intention to partner with leading local and global companies to establish AI centres of excellence.

According to Singapore's Ministry of Digital Development and Information, Deputy Prime Minister Lawrence Wong announced the investment in his Budget Statement on 16 February 2024: more than S$1 billion over the next five years into AI compute, talent and industry development. The National AI Strategy 2.0 itself had been launched in December 2023.

What is the GenAI Sandbox?

In a landmark move in line with its 2024 focus on AI, the Singapore government announced the inclusion of the SOMONITOR platform in the first batch of the GenAI Sandbox. The initiative reflects the nation's commitment to fostering innovation and supporting enterprises on their way to digital excellence.

Enterprise Singapore and the Infocomm Media Development Authority (IMDA) launched the sandbox for small and medium-sized enterprises on 7 February 2024. According to IMDA, about 300 SMEs from sectors including retail, food and beverage, education and hospitality were expected to benefit. Each SME could choose one generative AI solution to trial for three months, from two categories, "Marketing and Sales" and "Customer Engagement", and eligible successful applicants received grant support from IMDA.

What does SOMONITOR's inclusion mean for Singaporean enterprises?

SOMONITOR's inclusion in the GenAI Sandbox is not just a win for SOMIN, its parent company, but a step forward for Singaporean enterprises. The tool is built to collect, track and analyse competitors' marketing and industry-specific content at large scale. With government backing, SOMONITOR is set to change how businesses understand and interact with their market.

Why was SOMONITOR a fit for the GenAI Sandbox?

  • Advanced data collection and analysis: SOMONITOR's data collection is designed to leave no stone unturned in understanding market dynamics. Its analysis goes beyond numbers, giving businesses a historical account and a predictive model of competitor advertising and organic social media trends.

  • Tailored content insights: by focusing on industry-specific content, SOMONITOR makes sure insights are not broad strokes but tailored to the needs of individual enterprises. This specificity is crucial for nuanced marketing decisions. With SOMONITOR, KFC, say, would always know when anyone is advertising a chicken product, which audience they target, which promotions apply, which customer needs they appeal to and, in the end, which exact ads work and which do not.

  • Innovative use of LLMs and AI: SOMONITOR integrates large language models and SOMIN's AI for predicting the clickability of image content. These are at the heart of its ability to interpret and recommend content strategies, which made it an asset in the GenAI Sandbox.

  • Better decision-making: with SOMONITOR, businesses can make better-informed decisions about their marketing. Its ability to analyse content at scale and provide actionable insight can make a real difference to marketing strategy.

How much government support does the GenAI Sandbox provide?

As of 2024, the Singapore government supported 70% of enterprise expenses on SOMONITOR through the GenAI Sandbox initiative, a clear signal of its belief in the transformative power of AI. By backing such innovations, Singapore is investing not just in a company but in a smarter, more efficient future for its enterprises. For businesses, marketing no longer has to be guesswork: SOMONITOR's inclusion in the GenAI Sandbox gives companies access to AI-powered insights to drive their campaigns. The future of marketing is informed, strategic and data-driven.

As SOMONITOR enters the GenAI Sandbox, it carries the aspirations of many enterprises looking to carve out a niche in a competitive market. With the support of the Singapore government and the spirit of GenAI, SOMONITOR is more than a tool: it shows the path for businesses ready for the new era of marketing intelligence.

FAQ

What is Singapore's GenAI Sandbox?

It is a programme launched by Enterprise Singapore and IMDA on 7 February 2024 that lets SMEs trial a generative AI solution for three months, with grant support for eligible applicants. Solutions fall into two categories, marketing and sales, and customer engagement.

When did SOMONITOR join the GenAI Sandbox?

SOMONITOR was included in the first batch of the GenAI Sandbox in 2024, the year Singapore's Budget set out more than S$1 billion over five years for AI.

What does SOMONITOR do?

It collects, tracks and analyses competitors' marketing and industry-specific content, using large language models and SOMIN's clickability prediction AI. It shows which audiences competitors target, which promotions they run and which ads work.

How much support did enterprises receive?

As of 2024, the government supported 70% of enterprise expenses on SOMONITOR through the GenAI Sandbox. Check current terms with IMDA, as programme details can change.
