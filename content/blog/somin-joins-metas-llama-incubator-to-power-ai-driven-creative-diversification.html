We’re excited to share some big news: SOMIN has been selected to join Meta’s very first LLaMA Incubator program in Singapore.

For us, this isn’t just about being part of a cohort of AI startups. It’s about stepping into the future of how marketing is built, powered, and scaled with artificial intelligence - while keeping transparency and creativity at the core.

What Is the LLaMA Incubator?

Meta’s LLaMA (Large Language Model Meta AI) is one of the most advanced open-source AI models available today. It’s designed to understand and generate language at a scale that makes it useful for everything from customer service to research to creative ideation.

The LLaMA Incubator, launched in partnership with the Singapore government, is a six-month program that brings together a small group of startups across Southeast Asia to:

  • Experiment with LLaMA models in real-world applications,

  • Get mentorship and technical guidance from Meta’s AI experts,

  • Explore best practices in responsible AI, and

  • Share their work at a final Demo Day with investors, policymakers, and industry leaders on Oct 15th 2025.

In short: it’s Meta’s way of empowering the next generation of AI builders - and SOMIN is proud to be among them.

The Power of Diversification

One of the biggest shifts Meta is pushing in advertising right now is the move toward creative diversification.

Gone are the days when you could bet everything on a single “winning” ad. With Meta’s Andromeda engine, the focus is now on giving the algorithm a variety of creative options - different angles, messages, and formats - so it can serve the right message to the right audience.

At SOMIN, we’ve always believed in this approach. Our tools don’t just help marketers come up with one good idea; they help them generate a portfolio of ideas - each designed to speak to different customer motivations and contexts.

This isn’t about flooding platforms with content for the sake of it. It’s about strategic diversity, where every variation has a clear role and rationale. That’s the kind of thinking we’re excited to bring into the incubator.
