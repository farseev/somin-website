SOMIN Reporting is the performance-reporting solution from SOMIN (SoMin.ai), the AI marketing-intelligence company run by Somin Pte. Ltd. in Singapore. It connects a client's social accounts to the SOMONITOR engine and shows what is landing, broken into themes, in a live dashboard the client can open without waiting for a monthly deck. The live dashboard is coming soon.

What is SOMIN Reporting?

SOMIN Reporting is a live client report built on SOMONITOR. The SOMIN Reporting page describes it as the client's own reporting view: a dashboard that stays current, in the client's language, instead of a deck rebuilt by hand every month.

It is aimed at the team that owns the client relationship. SOMIN names three groups: the Head of Insights or Account Director who leads it, the account lead or analyst who runs it, and the clients and leadership who depend on it.

Is SOMIN Reporting available now?

Not yet as a finished product. SOMIN labels the solution "coming". The engine behind it runs today, and SOMIN says customers already cut reporting time on it. The live, self-serve dashboard on top is being productised, and SOMIN invites early-access requests.

That matters when you plan. If you need a client dashboard this week, ask SOMIN what is available for your account before you commit. This article describes the solution as SOMIN publishes it, and it does not promise a date.

How does SOMIN Reporting work?

It follows four steps, as listed on the SOMIN Reporting page:

  1. Connect the accounts. The client's social accounts connect through each platform's API.
  2. Forward into SOMONITOR. The data is forwarded into SOMONITOR and processed like all other category signal.
  3. A live, named dashboard. It surfaces topics, themes and category movement in named snapshots, such as "Q3 Report", in the client's own languages.
  4. Compare and export. It compares periods side by side, for example Q2 against Q3, and exports what you need. The report stays current between reviews.

The data source is the client's own social accounts. SOMIN does not describe other data sources for this solution, so check the scope with the team before you plan around one.

How is a themed report different from a normal social dashboard?

A normal dashboard counts things: volume, reach and sentiment. SOMIN Reporting breaks what is landing into themes, so a client can see which topics are working and not only how many people reacted. SOMIN's page lists three limits it wants to fix:

  • Generic social dashboards show volume, reach and sentiment.
  • A monthly report is out of date the day after it is built.
  • Analysts spend days each cycle assembling the same deck by hand.

In response, SOMIN says its report shows what is landing by theme, stays current between reviews, compares periods side by side in the client's language, and turns the reporting grind into a dashboard the client opens themselves.

What results has SOMIN published for reporting?

SOMIN cites one case: content agency Click2View, on SOMONITOR. According to the SOMIN Reporting page, the agency reached 9x faster reporting, and analysis time per cycle fell from 18 hours to 3 hours.

These are SOMIN's own published figures for the engine, not for the dashboard, which SOMIN says is still coming. Treat them as a customer example, not a guarantee for your team. Ask for a walkthrough on your own accounts before you rely on them.
