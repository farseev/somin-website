What is Cyber Week?

Cyber Week is the informal name for the stretch from Thanksgiving to Cyber Monday, and sometimes the days after it. Many online shops now start their offers earlier in November and keep them running past Monday. For ad planning, treat it as one season with three peaks: the early-access period, the Black Friday weekend and Cyber Monday itself.

Are local "Cyber" events on the same day?

Not always. In Latin America, searches such as "cyber 2027 fechas" usually refer to national online sales events. Chile's CyberDay is organised by the Santiago Chamber of Commerce, and Argentina's CyberMonday by the Argentine Chamber of Electronic Commerce (CACE). Their dates are announced by the organisers and do not follow the US calendar, so check the official event sites before you plan. In Asia, the main peaks are 11.11 and 12.12, covered in our APAC mega-sale calendar.

How should you plan Cyber Week ads?

Start early and split the season into phases.

  1. Six to eight weeks before: review what competitors ran last year and decide your offer. Look at which offers were common, so yours stands out.
  2. Four weeks before: build audiences. Warm up new customers with useful content, so you have people to retarget during the peak.
  3. Two weeks before: finish creative for each phase: teaser, main offer, last chance. Prepare more variations than usual, because creative tires faster when people see many ads.
  4. During the week: move budget towards the ads and audiences that convert, and keep a reserve for Cyber Monday.
  5. After: record what ran and what worked while the data is fresh, for next year.
