What does SOMIN do?

SOMIN is an AI marketing-intelligence company. Its SOMONITOR platform turns verified listening data on competitor ads, posts and creators into insights, briefs, pre-scored campaign concepts and on-brand content ideas. It serves agencies, brands and creator programs across 11+ markets in APAC, MENA and Europe.

The company's approach follows from his research. SOMIN's agents follow a fixed research process, and each output is traced to its source. You can see the product side on the SOMIN AI agents page.
