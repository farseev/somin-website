The SOMIN Social Media Manager is an AI agent from SOMIN (SoMin.ai), the AI marketing-intelligence company run by Somin Pte. Ltd. in Singapore. It watches what is landing in your category, writes each post in your account's voice, generates the image and publishes across your channels. You can run it fully autonomously or keep a human sign-off before anything goes live.

Key takeaways

  • It runs the whole social loop: find what is landing, write in your voice, make the image, publish.
  • Every post starts from SOMONITOR, SOMIN's real-time category engine, not from a blank prompt.
  • Control is a setting: fully autonomous, or a sign-off checkpoint where you approve, edit or skip each post.
  • SOMIN says it already runs SOMIN's own LinkedIn channel end to end.
  • SOMIN states that it can run alongside a scheduling or analytics suite, so it does not need to replace one.

What is the SOMIN Social Media Manager?

It is a SOMIN solution that handles the daily social loop. The SOMIN Social Media Manager page calls it "a manager that watches the category and shows up daily, in your voice", and contrasts it with a scheduler you have to feed.

SOMIN lists the status as Live. It is built for the team that owns the feed: the Head of Social or Content, the social media manager who runs it, and brand leadership who want a consistent voice without more headcount.

How does it work, step by step?

SOMIN describes four steps:

  1. Watch what is landing. It watches your category and breaks it into angles, tensions and themes worth posting into.
  2. Write in your voice. For each post it draws on thousands of category strategies to build a fresh angle, writes it in the account's own voice and generates a brand-aligned image.
  3. Compile and publish. It builds the calendar, schedules and publishes across every channel, not only one platform.
  4. Autonomous or gated. The loop runs on its own, or pauses for a human sign-off before anything goes live.

The aim is that a post enters the conversation as it is today, instead of repeating what the whole internet already says. For why that matters, see authentic content with AI.

How does it keep a brand on-brand?

Three controls do the work, per SOMIN's page: the voice of the account, a brand-aligned image for each post, and an optional human gate.

  • Voice. Posts are written in the account's own voice, not in a generic AI tone.
  • Image. The image is generated to be brand-aligned. SOMIN also shows on-brand creative landing in a brand's own Figma file in a demo clip on the page.
  • Gate. With the gate on, you approve, edit or skip each post. SOMIN states that autonomy is a setting, not a default you are stuck with.

Whatever you choose, keep your own checks. Our QA checklist for AI marketing content is a good start, because no tool removes the need to know what you have published.

What does SOMIN say about proof?

SOMIN gives two points. First, the Social Media Manager runs SOMIN's own LinkedIn channel, so it is not a demo. Second, the grounding model behind it was evaluated in a peer-reviewed study.

According to the SOMIN page, the grounding model scored 0.83 nDCG@10, a measure of retrieval accuracy, against 0.75 and 0.77 for ChatGPT and Gemini, listed in that order, on the same evaluation. SOMIN presents this as retrieving category content more accurately than general-purpose models. Note that nDCG@10 measures retrieval of category content, not post performance. It does not show that a given post will earn more engagement.

Who is it for, and who is it not for?

It suits lean teams that need a daily presence on several channels. It may be less useful if your plan is a small set of hand-written posts that you only need queued. In that case, SOMIN's own comparison points you to Buffer.

FAQ

Will the SOMIN Social Media Manager post off-brand?

SOMIN says it writes in your account's own voice and that you can keep a human sign-off gate where you approve, edit or skip each post before it publishes.

How is it different from Buffer or Hootsuite?

SOMIN says schedulers publish what you give them, while its manager finds what is landing, writes in your voice, makes the image and publishes. SOMIN's comparison page covers Buffer, Sprout Social and Jasper.

Does it just rewrite trending posts?

No, according to SOMIN. It grounds each post in what is resonating in your category through SOMONITOR, then writes a fresh angle in your voice.

Which channels does it publish to?

SOMIN says it is built to publish across every channel you run, not a single platform. The page does not list individual networks, so confirm yours with SOMIN.

Can I run it fully autonomously?

Yes, or with a sign-off checkpoint before each post. SOMIN states that it already runs its own LinkedIn channel end to end.
