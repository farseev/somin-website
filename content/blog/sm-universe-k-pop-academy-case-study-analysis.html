SM Universe is a K-pop training academy in Singapore. SOMIN (SoMin.ai), the AI marketing-intelligence company run by Somin Pte. Ltd. in Singapore, read 24 months of organic singing content for it. The main finding: singing content nearly tripled, the K-pop pathway is led by SM Universe, and vocal technique is the unclaimed territory that creators cover and academies do not.

Key takeaways

  • Singing content in the study grew from 74 to 213 items per window between mid-2024 and Q2 2026.
  • Vocal-technique content made up 79% of creators' singing content but only 4% of SM Universe's.
  • The K-pop training and audition pathway rose from 0% to 48% of the conversation, and SM Universe leads it.
  • Expression, confidence and being heard had 0% for SM Universe and all 11 rivals, and 21% for creators.
  • SOMIN turned the findings into three content pillars and a 39-post, 13-week calendar.

What is SM Universe?

SM Universe is a K-pop training academy in Singapore. The SOMIN case study describes six programmes, from K-pop vocal class to audition preparation. Its pathway content refers to the real SM Entertainment training system. This article covers only what SOMIN found in its study. It is not an official description of the academy.

What did SOMIN study?

SOMIN read organic singing content from three groups over 24 months, from June 2024 to June 2026. Paid content was out of scope.

  • SM Universe itself.
  • 11 Singapore academies, including K-Pop Vocal Coach, JR Academy, VOCA Academy and The Vocal Studio.
  • A panel of 10 creators, tracked apart from the brands.

The total was 1,306 organic singing items from 12 Singapore brands plus the creator panel. SOMIN split the 24 months into 12 bimonthly windows and checked each direction against the prior year. The primary persona was "The Aspiring Singer", a Singapore teen or young adult who treats the voice as identity.

What was the main trend?

The singing conversation nearly tripled. It went from 74 organic items per window in mid-2024 to 213 by Q2 2026. Two content themes led it: stage and access (51%) and the K-pop training and audition pathway (48%).

Two shifts stood out. The K-pop pathway went from 0% two years earlier to 48%, after SM Universe entered in August 2025. And vocal technique and coaching quality fell to 0% of academy content in the latest view, while creators made it 79% of theirs.

Where was the whitespace?

SOMIN scored four content territories by each group's own share of content, not raw counts. In the latest 90 days, SM Universe had 77 items, the 11 competitors 220 and the creator panel 118. Three territories became pillars. The fourth, the competitor-owned "stage and access" commons, stayed a defensive watch, because SM Universe held 0% there and staying out was right.

What three content pillars did SOMIN recommend?

The study built three pillars, each tied to one territory.

  1. Own the Craft (recover, lead): vocal-technique authority covering breath, range, runs, vocal health and honest coaching. The case notes that vocal-technique content already earned 7,172 engagement across 30 of 220 evidence posts, though SM Universe held only 4% of it.
  2. The Real Pathway (contested, brand-led): proof of the real credential through audition preparation on the actual SM Entertainment system. The pathway drew 172,249 engagement across 60 of 77 evidence posts since SM Universe's August 2025 entry.
  3. Every Voice Heard (emerging, test): first-stage moments about confidence, expression and community. The underlying content carried 2,710,301 engagement, and no academy had entered the space.

The case calls expression, confidence and being heard the clearest opening, where a brand's absence is true whitespace. It also marks it as emerging, so it should be tested with care.

What did SOMIN deliver?

SOMIN's deliverable was a brand briefing, a data-backed read of a category built from real organic content rather than surveys.

  • A 39-post content calendar over 13 weeks, from 6 July to 4 October 2026, posting on Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday.
  • A weighting of 20, 12 and 7 posts across the three pillars, favouring Own the Craft, timed around Singapore dates such as National Day.
  • A 72-piece creative library.
  • Nine live propositions across the three pillars, built through the chain persona, tension, angle, story and proposition.

The case page says this kind of work typically runs 13 times faster on SOMIN than manual research. That is SOMIN's own claim, so treat it as such. The method uses the same chain as in the persona, tension, angle and story framework. For the product behind it, see SOMIN strategy.

What can other brands learn from this case?

Three lessons carry beyond one academy. They are our reading, not claims from the case.

  • Compare shares, not counts, so a small brand and a large group can be read on the same scale.
  • Track creators apart from competitors. They show what independent voices publish, which can differ sharply from what brands do.
  • Check each trend against the prior year, so a seasonal bump is not mistaken for a shift.

FAQ

What is SM Universe?

SM Universe is a K-pop training academy in Singapore, and the subject of a SOMIN brand case study on singing content. This article only reports what the study found.

What did SOMIN find for SM Universe?

Singing content nearly tripled over 24 months. Vocal technique was left to creators (79% of their content, against 4% for SM Universe), the K-pop pathway was led by SM Universe, and expression and confidence was untouched by any academy.

What is a creator panel, and why track it separately?

It is a set of real creators posting to the same audience, tracked apart from brands. In this study the 10-creator panel showed what independent voices publish, which was mostly vocal craft.

What is in the SM Universe content calendar?

It has 39 posts over 13 weeks, from 6 July to 4 October 2026, on Tuesdays, Thursdays and Saturdays, with a 20, 12 and 7 split across the three pillars.

Did the study include paid ads?

No. The case says paid content is out of scope. It used organic singing posts only.
