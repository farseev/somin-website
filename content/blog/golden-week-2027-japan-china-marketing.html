When is Golden Week 2027 in Japan?

Golden Week is a cluster of national holidays. In 2027 the Cabinet Office lists Showa Day on 29 April, Constitution Memorial Day on 3 May, Greenery Day on 4 May and Children's Day on 5 May.

The calendar lays out like this:

  • Thursday 29 April: Showa Day (holiday)
  • Friday 30 April: working day, unless taken as leave
  • Saturday 1 and Sunday 2 May: weekend
  • Monday 3 to Wednesday 5 May: three national holidays

The law adds a substitute holiday if a national holiday falls on a Sunday, and a "citizens' holiday" when a day sits between two holidays. Neither rule applies in 2027, because none of the four holidays falls on a Sunday and 30 April sits between a holiday and a non-holiday. When is the 2027 May Day holiday in China?

It has not been announced in any source I opened. China's State Council issues the official holiday schedule, including make-up working days, and it does so ahead of the year. French readers search for this as "golden week Chine", and the answer is the same: wait for the notice.

For the pattern, look at 2026. The State Council notice, published by Xinhua on 4 November 2025, set Labour Day as a break from Friday 1 May to Tuesday 5 May, with Saturday 9 May a working day. That shows two things to plan for: the break can be five days, and a weekend day can be swapped to work.

How do Japan and China differ for marketers?

They differ in who is on holiday and for how long. Japan's break is fixed by law and is short. China's break is set each year and can be longer, with swapped working days.

How should you time campaigns around the break?

Run three phases. Reach people before they leave or settle in, stay light during the break, and re-engage when they return. This is a working framework, not a measured rule, so test it on your own data.

  1. Before. Publish trip, gift and home-project content in the weeks ahead. Launch early enough for ads to learn.
  2. During. Keep paid spend flexible. Use content that suits leisure and travel, and avoid launches that need work-hours attention.
  3. After. Plan a return-to-routine message and follow-up offers for people who showed interest but did not buy.

Set budget and creative in Japan first, because its dates are known. For China, draft the plan, hold the launch dates, and confirm when the notice is out.

What should you avoid?

Avoid treating the two markets as one region with one calendar. Avoid fixed launch dates in China before the notice, and avoid using stale holiday dates from last year's file.

  • Do not assume China's 2027 break matches 2026.
  • Do not reuse creative across both markets; read each audience's habits separately.

FAQ

What are the Golden Week 2027 dates in Japan?

Thursday 29 April (Showa Day) and Monday 3 to Wednesday 5 May (Constitution Memorial Day, Greenery Day, Children's Day). Saturday 1 and Sunday 2 May are the weekend.

Is 30 April 2027 a public holiday in Japan?

No. The Cabinet Office list for 2027 does not include 30 April, so it is a normal working day unless an employer or worker takes leave.

When is the 2027 Labour Day holiday in China?

Not yet announced in the sources I opened. The State Council sets it each year; the 2026 break ran 1 to 5 May, and the notice was published in November 2025.

Is Golden Week in China in May or October?

China has two long breaks: Labour Day in early May and National Day from 1 October. The 2026 National Day break ran 1 to 7 October, according to the State Council notice.
