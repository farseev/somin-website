What is SOMIN CRM?

It is SOMIN's solution for always-on email testing. The SOMIN CRM page promises to "know the winning email before you hit send". Instead of guessing subject lines, each email is angled from live category signal, so the message leans on what is resonating in your category and not on a generic template.

It targets the team that owns the list: the Head of CRM or Retention, the lifecycle manager who runs it, and email operations and analytics. SOMIN states that it is designed to be platform-agnostic, sourcing contacts from your CRM and building sequences in your sending platform.

How does SOMIN CRM test an email before sending?

The loop has seven steps on the SOMIN CRM page:

  1. Set the target. You give it an audience and a goal. Contacts come from your CRM.
  2. Generate the angles. Through SOMONITOR it generates thousands of distinct strategies per setup, varying message, offer, framing and timing.
  3. Approve the approach (gate 1). You review and approve the direction before anything is built.
  4. Build the sequences. It builds the approved angles as live sequences in your sending platform, with copy, splits, schedules and frequency caps.
  5. Approve the assets (gate 2). You review and approve the built sequences before a single send.
  6. Go live and write back. It sends, the agent watches and outcomes are written back to your CRM.
  7. Promote the winners. It reads which angles win, statistically and per audience, promotes them, generates the next round and loops.

Note the wording. "Test before you send" here means choosing and approving strategies and assets before launch, then testing live with real contacts and promoting winners. It is not a promise to predict a send result with no live data.

What safeguards does it have?

Because the tool writes to live email, SOMIN says its design leads with controls:

  • Two human approval gates. You sign off the approach, then the built sequences.
  • A kill switch. It pauses the whole loop in under a minute.
  • Write-back. Outcomes return to your CRM, so results sit with your records.
