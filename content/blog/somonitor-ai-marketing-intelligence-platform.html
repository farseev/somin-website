SOMONITOR is the real-time category engine inside SOMIN (SoMin.ai), the AI marketing-intelligence company run by Somin Pte. Ltd. in Singapore. It listens to the ads, posts and creators in a category, and SOMIN's AI agents work from that verified data to produce insights, briefs, pre-scored concepts and content ideas. The platform, SOMIN, and its engine, SOMONITOR, are two names for one product.

Key takeaways

  • SOMONITOR is the engine; SOMIN is the company and the platform built on it.
  • It listens to competitor ads and content on Meta, Google, TikTok, LinkedIn and YouTube.
  • Every SOMIN AI agent runs on SOMONITOR, not on a generic model that answers from the open web.
  • Outputs include briefs, persona and tension studies, campaign concepts, ad scoring and on-brand content ideas, each traced to its source.
  • Agents propose and people approve: nothing irreversible happens without a human sign-off.

What is SOMONITOR?

SOMONITOR is SOMIN's category engine. It reads the live conversation in a market — what competitors run, what creators post and what audiences respond to — so SOMIN's outputs reflect what is landing now, not what the web says in general.

The name appears in two ways. On SOMIN's AI agents page, every agent "runs on SOMONITOR". Under Singapore's IMDA SMEs Go Digital programme, SOMIN is listed as the solution SOMONITOR. Both refer to the same product from the same company.

What does the SOMIN platform do?

The platform turns competitor and category data into marketing decisions. According to SOMIN's company data, its features are:

  • Brand Tracker: daily competitor ad and content intelligence across Meta, Google, TikTok, LinkedIn and YouTube.
  • Perspective Studies: persona, tension, proposition and white-space analysis.
  • Expressions: AI-generated campaign concepts, briefs and on-brand visuals.
  • Ad and content scoring: predictive clickability before launch.
  • Agency support: pitch, reporting and strategy, with white-label deployment.
  • Creator tools: creator selection and sales-focused creator briefs.
  • Agentic research process: fixed steps with traceable sources.
  • Connections: Claude, Manus and Codex through MCP, and Zapier.

SOMIN states that a typical brief becomes scored ideas in about 13 minutes. See what creative clickability is for how pre-launch scoring works.

How is SOMONITOR different from social listening or a general AI assistant?

Listening tools report what is being said. SOMIN decodes what brands are doing: according to its Strategy page, every competitor ad is parsed for proposition, persona and tension, and the whitespace rivals leave open is mapped. General AI assistants answer from the open web, which is why SOMIN grounds its agents in category data instead.

SOMIN also says the engine can rule an angle out, not only confirm one. For a side-by-side with named tools, read the SOMIN comparison with ChatGPT, Perplexity, Meltwater and GWI, and social listening vs audience research vs ad optimisation.

Who uses the SOMIN platform?

Three groups, each with a page:

  • Advertising and media agencies, for pitching, reporting and strategy.
  • Brand marketing teams, for tracking competitors and briefing teams.
  • Creator and affiliate programmes, for selecting creators and writing sales-focused briefs.

SOMIN lists 11 markets across APAC, MENA and Europe. The engine's recognition by the Singapore government is covered in SOMONITOR joins the GenAI Sandbox.

How does a brief become scored ideas?

SOMIN states a fixed research process: listen to the category, decode what competitors do, find what is open, then generate and score concepts. On the Strategy agent, SOMIN reports a median of 13 minutes from brief to pre-scored concepts, and points to Google Cloud's customer story on SOMIN as documentation. The result can be a client-ready deck in the client's own brand.

The same engine feeds the other agents. Paid Media predicts click-through before spend, Influencer & GMV briefs creators on what lifts sales, and Social Media Manager writes on-trend posts in your voice. SOMIN's Marketing Mondays plugin also exposes the data to Claude; see the plugin explained.

How is the output kept explainable?

SOMIN says every recommendation traces to its source data, using an explainable-AI layer on top of the language model. Its AI agents page names Gemini 2.5 Pro on Google Cloud for ad understanding and idea generation, and SOMIN's own SoCraft models for click-through prediction. The research is published at ACM venues; the list is on the research page.

Control stays with people. On the AI agents page, Paid Media ads wait paused until you approve spend, CRM & Email needs two approvals before sending, and Social runs autonomously only by choice.
