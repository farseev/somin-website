In the dynamic world of digital marketing, staying informed about what people are saying about your brand and industry online is crucial. Social listening tools are invaluable for this purpose. They enable businesses to monitor, track, and analyze conversations across social media platforms, identify trends, track campaign success, and understand competitive landscapes. In 2024, the variety and capabilities of these tools have grown immensely. This comprehensive guide explores the best social listening tools available, along with a special focus on a groundbreaking tool, SOMONITOR.

Understanding Social Listening Tools

Social listening tools are specialized software designed to monitor digital conversations. They scan social media platforms for mentions of specific brands, keywords, phrases, or hashtags. This technology is not just about tracking mentions; it's about gaining insights into customer perceptions, identifying potential leads, and understanding market trends.

The 11 Traditional Social Listening Tools to watch 2024

  • Brand24: Known for its real-time monitoring and diverse source coverage, Brand24 is a comprehensive tool for tracking online conversations. It features an intuitive dashboard, sentiment analysis, and advanced analytics.

  • Brandwatch: This enterprise-level tool provides geolocation, image recognition, demographic data, and a powerful data visualization platform, Vizia.

  • XPro (Tweetdeck): Focused exclusively on Twitter, XPro is excellent for managing multiple profiles and tracking Twitter-specific activities.

  • Awario: A budget-friendly option, Awario offers essential monitoring across multiple platforms, excluding Telegram and TikTok.

  • Buffer: Primarily a social media management platform, Buffer requires an additional subscription for its listening feature, currently limited to Twitter.

  • Sprout Social: A combination of social media management and listening, Sprout Social is great for tracking brand mentions across various platforms.

  • Keyhole: Notable for its hashtag tracking ability, Keyhole provides in-depth analytics and monitoring across multiple social networks.

  • Talkwalker: Offering access to historical data and real-time monitoring, Talkwalker is a robust tool for tapping into customer discussions.

  • Hootsuite: Partnering with Brandwatch, Hootsuite focuses on trends, topics, and profile monitoring, offering insightful audience insights.

  • BuzzSumo: Ideal for content marketers, BuzzSumo tracks keywords and alerts while providing a comprehensive view of content performance.

  • HubSpot: An all-in-one marketing platform, HubSpot excels in lead nurturing and offers keyword monitoring streams.
