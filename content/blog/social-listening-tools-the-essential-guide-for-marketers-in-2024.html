What do social listening tools do?

Social listening tools are specialised software designed to monitor digital conversations. They scan social media platforms for mentions of specific brands, keywords, phrases or hashtags. The technology is not just about tracking mentions; it is about gaining insight into customer perceptions, identifying potential leads and understanding market trends.

It helps to separate two jobs that often share one tool. Social monitoring is reactive: it catches individual mentions so a team can reply, resolve a complaint or spot a crisis early. Social listening is analytical: it looks at the pattern across thousands of mentions over time to answer bigger questions, such as how sentiment towards a brand is shifting, which topics are rising in a category or what customers complain about most.

Most tools share a core feature set: keyword or Boolean queries that define what to track, sentiment analysis, alerts, dashboards and reports, share-of-voice comparisons with competitors and some way to find influential authors. Where they differ is in which sources they cover, how far back their data goes and how well their analysis handles local languages and slang.

How do you choose a social listening tool?

Start from the questions you need answered, then check each tool against them:

  • Coverage: which social networks, news sites, forums, blogs and review sites it tracks. Access to some platforms depends on their APIs and changes over time.
  • Languages and markets: for brands working across Asia, sentiment analysis has to cope with several languages and mixed-language posts.
  • Historical data: whether you can look back before the day you set up the query, which matters for benchmarks and trend analysis.
  • Organic and paid: most tools focus on organic conversation; few show what competitors are running as ads.
  • Pricing model: plans are often priced by seats, queries or mention volume, so estimate your volume before comparing prices.

Which 11 established social listening tools did we review in 2024?

These were the 11 traditional social listening tools we suggested watching in 2024. Descriptions reflect each tool as of 2024.

  • Brand24: known for real-time monitoring and wide source coverage, Brand24 is a comprehensive tool for tracking online conversations. It features an intuitive dashboard, sentiment analysis and advanced analytics.

  • Brandwatch: this enterprise-level tool provides geolocation, image recognition, demographic data and a powerful data visualisation platform, Vizia.

  • XPro (TweetDeck): focused exclusively on Twitter (now X), XPro is excellent for managing multiple profiles and tracking Twitter-specific activity.

  • Awario: a budget-friendly option, Awario offers essential monitoring across multiple platforms, excluding Telegram and TikTok (as of 2024).

  • Buffer: primarily a social media management platform, Buffer requires an additional subscription for its listening feature, which was limited to Twitter (as of 2024).

  • Sprout Social: a combination of social media management and listening, Sprout Social is well suited to tracking brand mentions across various platforms.

  • Keyhole: notable for hashtag tracking, Keyhole provides in-depth analytics and monitoring across multiple social networks.

  • Talkwalker: offering access to historical data and real-time monitoring, Talkwalker is a robust tool for tapping into customer discussions.

  • Hootsuite: partnering with Brandwatch (as of 2024), Hootsuite focuses on trends, topics and profile monitoring, offering useful audience insights.

  • BuzzSumo: ideal for content marketers, BuzzSumo tracks keywords and alerts while giving a comprehensive view of content performance.

  • HubSpot: an all-in-one marketing platform, HubSpot excels at lead nurturing and offers keyword monitoring streams.

FAQ

What is the difference between social listening and social monitoring?

Monitoring tracks individual mentions so you can respond to them. Listening analyses the patterns across many mentions over time to understand customer perceptions, trends and the competitive landscape.

Which social listening tool suits a small budget?

In our 2024 review, Awario was the budget-friendly option, offering essential monitoring across multiple platforms. Check current coverage before you buy, because it excluded Telegram and TikTok at the time.
