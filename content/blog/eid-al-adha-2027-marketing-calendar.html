Key takeaways

  • Eid al-Adha follows the Hajj season and is about family, sacrifice, sharing and charity.

How should you plan campaigns?

  1. Six to eight weeks before: agree the message. Family, generosity and sharing fit the occasion; heavy discount messaging can feel out of place during the days of Eid itself.
  2. Four weeks before: finish creative in each local language and check imagery with local teams. Avoid casual use of religious symbols.
  3. Two weeks before: run travel, gifting, fashion and food campaigns as people prepare.
  4. During Eid: lighter, greeting-style content; many offices close, so B2B messages wait.
  5. After: back-to-routine and summer travel messages.

FAQ

When is Eid al-Adha 2027?

It is expected on 16 or 17 May 2027, depending on the moon sighting. The Day of Arafat is expected on 15 or 16 May.

Why can the date differ between countries?

Islamic months start with the sighting of the new moon, and countries may confirm the sighting on different days.

What is Hari Raya Haji?

The name for Eid al-Adha in Singapore and Malaysia. It is a public holiday in both countries.

How early should brands plan?

Six to eight weeks ahead for creative and media, with both possible dates in the plan.
